September 4, 2026 · Fashion Social Commerce
Fashion Social Commerce & Creator Commerce — Full Market Report
  • Winning model is curation infrastructure, not a "social network with a shop" — LTK and ShopMy, the category's two biggest successes, sit between brands and creators and monetize via SaaS/affiliate, not an ad-supported feed.
  • Pure content feeds with shopping bolted on keep failing — Flip, Amazon Inspire, 21 Buttons, and Roposo Clout all shut down. Working formats need a transactional anchor: scarcity (Whatnot auctions), unique inventory (Vinted/Depop/Poshmark), or a wardrobe utility.
  • Live shopping only scales in the West as scarcity-driven auctions — Whatnot hit $8B GMV in 2025 (~60% of the $22B North America + Europe live-shopping market), not as a "TV shopping" feed.
  • Resale is now structural, not niche — Vinted GMV €10.8B (+47%), Depop sold to eBay for ~$1.2B, ThredUp projects global secondhand at $393B by 2030.
  • Meta fully dropped native in-app checkout in 2025 and relaunched Instagram affiliate links in Reels (March 2026, 1,000+ followers) — mega-platforms keep exiting the in-app marketplace role in favor of AI discovery.
  • TikTok Shop is the one Western social-commerce channel working at real scale ($64.3B global GMV, +94%); 69.9% of its EU GMV comes from affiliate creators, not catalog listings — but EU5 seller commission just rose from 5% to 9% (Jan 2026), opening room for alternatives.
  • Digital wardrobe is the product layer closest to SMTHG's mechanics — Whering ($7M seed, 10M users) and Alta Daily ($11M seed, 20M+ garment images processed) both raised from eBay Ventures, Google AI Futures Fund, and LVMH's Aglaé.
  • Realistic seed target for SMTHG per current comps: $3–10M (Whering $7M, Alta Daily $11M) — $30M+ rounds are going only to companies with proven GMV.
  • "AI stylist" stopped being a differentiator by mid-2026 — LTK, Meta, Pinterest, and Amazon all shipped one. The report's read: the defensible layer is the underlying data source (catalog, creator content, personal wardrobe, or community) — community is the position nobody else on the list occupies.

September 4, 2026 · Digital Wardrobe
Digital Wardrobe Market Report
  • Digital wardrobe became its own investment category in 2025–2026 — the closest product layer to SMTHG, since it monetizes a utility (cataloging clothes) rather than a feed.
  • Funding stage is still early across the board: Whering, Alta Daily, Save Your Wardrobe, and Indyx are all at seed or pre-seed — none has publicly closed a Series A yet.
  • Whering leads on scale: 10M users, mostly Gen Z; $7M seed (July 2026) from eBay Ventures and Google AI Futures Fund.
  • Alta Daily processed 20M+ garment images using Meta's Segment Anything model, cutting per-image segmentation cost to a few cents; $11M seed from Menlo Ventures, LVMH's Aglaé, and others.
  • Engagement doesn't automatically convert to revenue — Indyx catalogued 10M+ items but is reported to earn only ~$10K/month, echoing the same feed-without-monetization pattern seen in Flip.
  • Save Your Wardrobe pivoted from a consumer app to B2B (repair/resale infrastructure for brands) after its 2022 seed — a signal that pure B2C digital-wardrobe apps struggle to self-sustain.
  • Market-size estimates are wildly inconsistent across analyst firms — $250M to $3.67B for the same 2025–2026 base year. The more conservative estimates cluster around $245–273M with 9–16% CAGR.
  • The real investment thesis isn't "wardrobe organizer app" — investor composition (eBay, Google, LVMH-linked Aglaé) points to wardrobe data as infrastructure for resale and retail media, with monetization shifting toward B2B data licensing over consumer subscriptions.
