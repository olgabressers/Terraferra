1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device. They can be set by us ("first-party cookies") or by a service provider we use ("third-party cookies").

We may also use similar technologies, such as local storage, where they perform the same function as cookies. This Policy refers to all of these technologies as "cookies."

2. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly Necessary Cookies are essential to provide the Website and the features you request. They may be used to:

  • remember your cookie choices;
  • protect the Website and its visitors against abuse or security threats; and
  • support the technical delivery of the Website.

These cookies are always active in our cookie preference manager. You can block them through your browser settings, but doing so may prevent parts of the Website from working correctly.

3. Performance Cookies

With your permission, we use Performance Cookies to understand how visitors use the Website and to improve its performance and content. Refusing Performance Cookies does not affect your access to the Website.

We do not set Performance Cookies until you select Accept analytics in our cookie banner or enable the Analytics category in Cookie Settings. You may withdraw or change your choice at any time through the Cookie Settings link in the Website footer.

5. Managing Cookies

When you first visit the Website, our cookie banner lets you accept or reject optional Performance Cookies, or open Cookie Settings to make a granular choice. Strictly Necessary Cookies remain active because they are required to provide the Website and save your choice.

You can revisit your choice at any time through the Cookie Settings link in the Website footer. Changing your choice takes effect for future cookie activity. You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Browser controls may not remove cookies that were previously set, and blocking Strictly Necessary Cookies may affect Website functionality.
