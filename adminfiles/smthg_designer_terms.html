2. Becoming a Designer on SMTHG

2.1 Application W&B-derived

Designer applies via the sign-up process and provides accurate business information (legal name, registered address, tax registration status, company number) on request.

2.2 Term W&B-derived

The Agreement takes effect once SMTHG accepts the application, and continues for the Subscription Term, auto-renewing for equal-length Renewal Periods unless terminated (Section 3).

3. Suspension & Termination

3.1 Suspension of a Drop W&B-derived

SMTHG may suspend or restrict a Drop if product information is inaccurate, or the listing breaches this Agreement, SMTHG's policies, or the law.

3.2 Grounds for Termination by SMTHG W&B-derived

  • Non-trivial or repeated breach of this Agreement
  • Unpaid invoice past due date
  • Designer becomes insolvent or its ability to fulfil orders is at risk
  • Continuing the relationship could expose SMTHG to reputational harm
  • SMTHG decides to stop operating SMTHG or stop selling the Designer's product category
  • Tax authority notice that Designer isn't meeting tax obligations
  • Breach of the Code of Ethics (Section 22)
  • SMTHG's own discretion

3.3 Notice Period W&B-derived

SMTHG gives 30 days' notice of termination, except where a legal/regulatory obligation, fraud, unsafe/counterfeit products, or repeated breach requires immediate termination.

3.4 Designer-Initiated Termination W&B-derived

Designer may terminate on 30 days' notice, effective at the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

3.5 Effect of Termination W&B-derived

  • The Platform Fee for the then-current term remains payable
  • No Fees already paid are refunded
  • Any amount owed by Designer to SMTHG remains payable; a late-payment fee applies after 7 days unpaid Not decided
  • SMTHG removes the Designer's listings within 14 days
  • No compensation or indemnity is payable by SMTHG to the Designer on termination or expiry

4. The Services

4.1 Service Level W&B-derived

SMTHG provides the Designer Dashboard and Drop infrastructure with reasonable skill and care, and aims for 24/7 availability subject to routine/emergency maintenance.

4.2 Curation W&B-derived

SMTHG uses reasonable endeavours to keep So-Me-Space a consistent standard of design quality across designers.

4.3 Changes to Services W&B-derived

SMTHG may change the Services on 30 days' written notice. Designer may terminate if it objects within that window.

5. Designer Obligations

5.1 Account Security W&B-derived

Designer is responsible for password/account security, must notify SMTHG immediately of any suspected unauthorized access, and keeps Dashboard content confidential.

5.2 No Circumvention W&B-derived

Designer must not include its own contact details, website links, or any means for a Customer to communicate or transact outside SMTHG, in listings or shipments.

5.3 Product Information & Conduct W&B-derived

Designer keeps product information accurate and current, abides by the Code of Ethics, and must not expose SMTHG to reputational harm.

5.4 Pricing W&B-derived

Designer sets the retail price, after consultation with SMTHG where SMTHG's curation/quality-mix requirements are relevant. Designer may not price the same product lower on its own site or other direct channels than on SMTHG.

6. Drops & Pre-Order Model

6.1 Pre-Order, Made-to-Order SMTHG-specific

SMTHG holds no physical product. Production starts only once the Drop window closes and orders are confirmed. Designer sets the Drop window, item count, and Expected Ship Date measured from Drop close.

7. Fees

7.1 Onboarding & Platform Fee SMTHG-specific

$250 one-time onboarding fee at sign-up, plus $500/month recurring Platform Fee. Founding Designers: $0 onboarding fee, $250/month Platform Fee.

7.2 Platform Commission SMTHG-specific

30% of GMV on every sale. This is a single bundled commission covering SMTHG's share and all influencer involvement — do not show the Designer a separate platform-vs-influencer breakdown.

7.3 Non-Fulfilment Fee W&B-derived Not decided

W&B charges this for late fulfillment, pre-fulfillment cancellation, or wrong item shipped. SMTHG needs an equivalent — rate not set.

7.4 Fee Changes W&B-derived

SMTHG may change Fees with 15 days' notice; Designer may terminate within that window if it objects.

7.5 Late Payment W&B-derived Not decided

W&B charges 4% over the Bank of England base rate on overdue sums. SMTHG needs a US-equivalent rate — not set.

7.6 Additional Influencer Agreements SMTHG-specific

If the Designer wants to enter into an additional or custom agreement with an influencer beyond what the standard Platform Commission already covers, an additional fee applies, agreed case-by-case.

8. Statement & Payment to Designer

8.1 Monthly Statement W&B-derived

Within 21 days of each calendar month's end, SMTHG posts a Statement on the Dashboard: GMV, Fees, and net amount due per Drop.

8.2 Payout Timing SMTHG-specific

SMTHG releases payout for an order only after both the Return Period and the Chargeback Period for that order have expired, whichever is later. Held funds are shown on the Statement as pending, not paid. This excludes any amount or order still subject to an open chargeback or dispute — that amount remains withheld until the dispute is resolved, even after the Return Period and Chargeback Period have otherwise expired.

8.3 Processor SMTHG-specific

Payments are processed via Stripe. Stripe's standard payment processing fee is included in the Platform Fee — it is not charged to the Designer separately.

8.4 Return Processing Fee SMTHG-specific

Unlike standard payment processing, return processing costs are charged to the Designer — a fee applies per returned order, as set out on the Designer Dashboard.

8.5 Withholding for Risk W&B-derived

SMTHG may withhold payout, or offset amounts owed, where it determines a Designer's account presents fraud, chargeback, or dispute risk.

8.6 Recovering a Shortfall SMTHG-specific

If a chargeback, dispute, or refund obligation exceeds the amount SMTHG then holds or owes the Designer, SMTHG may invoice the Designer directly for the difference, deduct it from a future Statement, or charge the payment method on file for the Designer's account.

9. Sale of Products

9.1 Merchant of Record W&B-derived Not decided

W&B acts as an "undisclosed agent" and merchant of record for UK VAT purposes. Whether SMTHG adopts an equivalent structure, or a simpler marketplace-facilitator model, isn't decided.

9.2 Designer Warranties W&B-derived

Designer warrants it owns/has rights to the products and their IP, is authorized to sell them, and that the products are safe, of satisfactory quality, and comply with applicable law.

9.3 Regulatory Compliance Stays with Designer W&B-derived

Nothing in this Agreement shifts product-safety, labelling, or manufacturing compliance obligations to SMTHG. Designer provides compliance documentation on request.

10. Delivery of Products

10.1 Designer Ships Direct SMTHG-specific

Designer ships every order directly to the Customer within a set number of business days of Drop close. SMTHG holds no stock and offers no store-fulfilment option.

10.2 Packing Slip W&B-derived

Each shipment includes an order-specific packing slip identifying the Designer as seller and as the party to which the Customer may return the product.

11. Returns & Refunds

11.1 Commission Clawback Model SMTHG-specific

Returns are handled financially via commission clawback on the Platform Commission — there is no physical return path to SMTHG.

12. Customer Chargebacks

12.1 Brand Risk SMTHG-specific

If a Customer files a chargeback for any reason related to the Designer's product or its delivery, that is the Designer's risk. SMTHG defends the chargeback through its integrated dispute system; if the card issuer rules against SMTHG, the resulting loss passes to the Designer.

12.2 Platform-Caused Chargebacks SMTHG-specific

Chargebacks caused by a platform issue are covered by SMTHG, not the Designer. Platform issues include: a duplicate or incorrect charge caused by a checkout error; a payment authorization or fraud-screening failure on SMTHG's side; SMTHG's failure to process an approved refund; or an order that failed to reach the Designer because of a technical fault in SMTHG's system.

13. Data Protection

13.1 Roles W&B-derived

SMTHG is controller; Designer is processor for Customer order/delivery data received to fulfil a shipment.

13.2 Use Restrictions W&B-derived

Designer uses Customer data only to fulfil the order, does not solicit the Customer directly or include outside promotional material in the shipment, and securely deletes the data 30 days after dispatch.

13.3 Breach Penalty W&B-derived Not decided

W&B charges £1,000 per breach of the no-solicitation clause. SMTHG needs a USD-equivalent figure — not set.

14. Intellectual Property

14.1 Designer Retains Design IP W&B-derived

SMTHG acknowledges the IP in the Designer's products and materials belongs to the Designer.

14.2 License to SMTHG W&B-derived

Designer grants SMTHG a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, reproduce, and publish product images/descriptions for listing, marketing, and So-Me-Space content.

14.3 Photography Ownership Not decided

Needs a clear rule on who owns product photography when SMTHG or a partner shoots it — not yet decided.

15. Indemnity & Liability

15.1 Designer Indemnifies SMTHG W&B-derived Not decided

Designer indemnifies SMTHG against losses from breach of this Agreement, product defects, IP infringement claims, or third-party injury/property claims arising from its products. Exact scope/caps not set.

15.2 Limitation of Liability W&B-derived Not decided

W&B caps its own aggregate liability to the seller at £1,000 (excluding fraud, death/personal injury). SMTHG needs an equivalent cap, in USD — not set.

16. Force Majeure

16.1 W&B-derived

Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control (natural disaster, civil unrest, infrastructure failure).

17. Sales Tax

17.1 US Sales Tax SMTHG-specific

SMTHG, as marketplace facilitator, collects and remits sales tax on sales to US Customers made through the platform — calculated per order based on the Customer's delivery address, via SMTHG's payment processor's tax engine. This is not a cost borne by the Designer.

17.2 EU/UK Customer Sales SMTHG-specific

SMTHG collecting payment on-platform for a sale to an EU/UK Customer makes SMTHG the VAT deemed supplier for that sale — a separate constraint on which routes are in scope at MVP.

18. Confidentiality

18.1 W&B-derived

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential, disclosing only to those who need it to perform this Agreement or as required by law.

19. Dispute Resolution

19.1 Mediation Before Litigation W&B-derived

Either party may request mediation via a preferred independent mediator before escalating a dispute.

19.2 Binding Arbitration; Class Action Waiver SMTHG-specific

If mediation does not resolve the dispute, it will be resolved by binding arbitration, on an individual basis. Neither party may bring a claim as a class, collective, or representative action, and the arbitrator may not consolidate more than one party's claims.

20. Governing Law

20.1 Delaware/US Law SMTHG-specific Not decided

W&B's terms are governed by English law with exclusive jurisdiction in England & Wales, reflecting its UK incorporation. SMTHG INC. is a Delaware C-corp — this Agreement would default to Delaware/US governing law and jurisdiction — not finalized.

20.2 Contracting Entity Not decided

Whether SMTHG needs a separate contracting entity for EU-based designers (as W&B does — separate US, EU, AU, and UK/rest-of-world entities) is a distinct, open question, not resolved by this page.

21. General

21.1 Amendments W&B-derived

SMTHG may amend these Terms on 30 days' notice, except for regulatory or urgent security/fraud reasons. Designer may terminate before the change takes effect if it disagrees; continued use after that date is acceptance.

21.2 Assignment W&B-derived

Designer may not assign its rights/obligations without SMTHG's consent. SMTHG may assign freely.

21.3 Entire Agreement W&B-derived

This Agreement (plus incorporated policies) is the entire agreement between the parties, superseding prior discussions.
