smthg. Designer Terms & Conditions

Draft seller agreement between SMTHG and designers/brands. Adapted from Wolf & Badger's seller terms (closest real-world comparable — curated indie-designer marketplace, seller ships direct) and Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement (fulfillment/liability language), rewritten for SMTHG's pre-order drop model and commercial terms.

1. Parties & Scope

1.1 Contracting Entity Placeholder

This Agreement is between SMTHG INC. (Delaware C-corp) and the Designer named at sign-up. Whether SMTHG needs a separate contracting entity for EU-based designers (as Wolf & Badger does — a Delaware entity for US sellers, an Ireland entity for EU sellers) is a separate, open corporate-structure question — not resolved by this page. Confirm with US startup counsel before this clause is finalized.

1.2 What This Covers SMTHG-specific

Applies to any designer/brand listing products for sale through SMTHG drops. Does not cover influencer agreements, consumer/buyer terms, or advertising add-ons — each gets its own document (see Related Documents below).

2. Onboarding

2.1 Onboarding Fee SMTHG-specific

$250 one-time onboarding fee, payable at sign-up. Source: MVP Costs / Budget.

2.2 Platform Fee SMTHG-specific

$500/month recurring platform fee, in addition to the commission in Section 4. Source: MVP Costs.

2.3 Designer Warranties Amazon BSA-derived

Designer confirms it owns or has the right to sell each product listed, and that nothing is counterfeit, stolen, or subject to a third party's claim.

3. Product Listings & Drops

3.1 Pre-Order Model SMTHG-specific

Unlike Wolf & Badger (continuous in-stock listings) or Amazon (ship-by-date on any order any day), SMTHG runs fixed-window drops. Products are made-to-order — production starts only once the drop window closes and orders are confirmed. Designer sets the drop window, item count, and Expected Ship Date (from drop close, not from order date).

3.2 Pricing W&B-derived

Designer sets the retail price, after consultation with SMTHG where SMTHG's experience and quality-mix requirements for the platform are relevant. SMTHG does not set price unilaterally.

3.3 Curation W&B-derived

SMTHG maintains editorial discretion over which designers and drops appear on the platform, to keep a consistent standard of design quality across So-Me-Space.

4. Fees & Commission

4.1 Platform Commission SMTHG-specific

10% of GMV on every sale, deducted before payout. Source: MVP Costs.

4.2 Influencer Program Fee SMTHG-specific

10% standard / 20% Founding Creator tier, charged on GMV attributed to that influencer's drop involvement, paid out to the influencer. Source: MVP Costs.

4.3 Non-Fulfilment / Late Cancellation Fee W&B-derived Placeholder

Wolf & Badger charges a fee when a seller is late fulfilling or cancels a confirmed sale. SMTHG should have an equivalent — exact fee/percentage not yet set, needs a business decision before this clause is final.

5. Payment

5.1 Processor SMTHG-specific

Payments processed via Stripe. Standard processing fee (2.9% + $0.30/order) applies against gross sale proceeds before payout. Source: Budget.

5.2 Payout Schedule W&B-derived Placeholder

Modeled on Wolf & Badger's pattern (payout within 7 days of statement date) as a starting point. SMTHG's actual payout cadence has not been decided — confirm before this becomes binding.

5.3 Withholding for Risk Amazon BSA-derived

SMTHG may withhold payout, or offset amounts owed, if it determines a designer's account presents a fraud, chargeback, or dispute risk.

6. Fulfillment & Shipping

6.1 Designer Ships Direct Amazon BSA-derived

Designer sources, packages, and ships every order directly to the customer — SMTHG holds no inventory. Designer packages products in a commercially reasonable manner complying with applicable packaging/labeling law, and ships by the Expected Ship Date (Section 3.1).

6.2 Packing Slip & Identification Amazon BSA-derived

Each shipment includes an order-specific packing slip identifying the designer as seller and as the party to which the customer may return the product.

7. Returns & Refunds

7.1 Commission Clawback Model SMTHG-specific

Unlike Wolf & Badger (returns fee as % of chargeable price) or Amazon (physical returns processed by the seller or via FBA), SMTHG has no physical return path — returns are handled financially through commission clawback on the SMTHG platform fee. Source: Architecture.

8. Problems, Defects & Liability

8.1 Designer Responsibility Amazon BSA-derived

Designer is solely responsible for non-delivery, misdelivery, defects, or recalls of its products, except where caused by SMTHG's own system failure. Designer notifies SMTHG promptly of any product recall.

8.2 Indemnification Amazon BSA-derived Placeholder

Designer indemnifies SMTHG against claims arising from its products. Exact scope/caps need counsel drafting — not a clause to finalize from a template alone.

9. Intellectual Property

9.1 Designer Retains Design IP SMTHG-specific

Designer retains all IP in its designs and products. Designer grants SMTHG a license to use product images and descriptions for marketing, the drop listing, and So-Me-Space content.

9.2 Photography Ownership W&B-derived Placeholder

Needs a clear rule on who owns product photography when SMTHG or a partner shoots it (a gap the PuppetVendors consignment template flags too) — not yet decided.

10. Term & Termination

10.1 Termination W&B-derived

Either party may terminate. No compensation or indemnity is payable by SMTHG to the designer on termination or expiry (matching Wolf & Badger's position that the relationship is not a commercial-agency relationship under agency-compensation law).

10.2 Wind-Down Period W&B-derived

Any outstanding balance owed to the designer is paid out within a wind-down period following termination, consistent with normal payout timing (Section 5.2).
