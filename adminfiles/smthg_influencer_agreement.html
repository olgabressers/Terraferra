This Agreement is between SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG"), and the individual or entity participating as an Influencer in the SMTHG Community ("Influencer").

1. Relationship Between the Parties SMTHG — added

Unlike a pure discovery tool where the platform is not a party to the arrangement, SMTHG is a direct party to this Agreement. SMTHG pays Influencer compensation directly, out of the platform commission SMTHG collects from Designer sales — Influencer is not paid by, and has no direct contractual relationship with, the Designer.

Influencer must clearly and conspicuously disclose every sponsored or compensated post involving SMTHG or a Designer's products, every time, as required by the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). This obligation is not covered anywhere in Shopify's Collabs Terms and is added here to satisfy US law.
