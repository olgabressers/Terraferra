This Agreement is between SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG"), and the individual or entity participating as an Influencer in the SMTHG Community ("Influencer").

1. Relationship Between the Parties SMTHG — added

Unlike a pure discovery tool where the platform is not a party to the arrangement, SMTHG is a direct party to this Agreement. SMTHG pays Influencer compensation directly, out of the platform commission SMTHG collects from Designer sales — Influencer is not paid by, and has no direct contractual relationship with, the Designer.

2. Independent Contractor Status Shopify Collabs — adapted

Influencer is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of SMTHG. Influencer is solely responsible for their own tax obligations, including self-employment tax, on any compensation received under this Agreement.

3. Scope of Collaboration SMTHG — added

Each collaboration is defined by a brief agreed between SMTHG and Influencer, covering the products involved, deliverables (e.g. posts, stories, reviews), and timeline. This Agreement governs the relationship generally; a brief governs each specific collaboration.

4. Content License Shopify Collabs — adapted, narrowed

Influencer retains ownership of the content they create. Influencer grants SMTHG a worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, display, and distribute that content for the purpose of operating, marketing, and promoting SMTHG and the relevant collaboration, including on the Website, the Community, and SMTHG's own social media accounts. Unlike Shopify Collabs' own terms, this license is not perpetual or irrevocable — its term is the duration of the collaboration plus [insert archive/marketing-use period], after which SMTHG will stop new use of the content on request.

5. Product Claims & Designer Relationship SMTHG — added

Influencer may only describe a Designer's product using information provided or approved by SMTHG or the Designer, and must not make claims about price, availability, materials, or performance that are not accurate. Influencer is not authorized to make commitments, representations, or promises on behalf of any Designer, and has no direct contractual relationship with the Designer (Section 1).

6. Third-Party Platforms Shopify Collabs — base

Influencer's use of any third-party platform (including Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube) to post content is governed by that platform's own terms. SMTHG is not responsible for the availability, performance, or policies of any third-party platform.

7. FTC Endorsement Disclosure SMTHG — added

Influencer must clearly and conspicuously disclose every sponsored or compensated post involving SMTHG or a Designer's products, every time, as required by the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). Where the platform provides a built-in disclosure tool (e.g. Instagram's Paid Partnership label, TikTok's Branded Content toggle), Influencer must use it in addition to an in-caption disclosure. This obligation is not covered anywhere in Shopify's Collabs Terms and is added here to satisfy US law.

9. Refunds, Chargebacks & Fraud SMTHG — added

If a Sale attributed to Influencer is later cancelled, refunded, or successfully charged back, the Commission on that Sale is reversed — deducted from Influencer's next payout, or invoiced separately if no future payout is available to offset it. SMTHG may withhold or reverse Commission, and may immediately terminate this Agreement (Section 15), where SMTHG reasonably believes Influencer engaged in fraud, fake engagement, or artificially inflated sales.

10. Brand Safety SMTHG — added

Influencer will not associate SMTHG or a Designer's products with illegal content, sexually explicit content, hate speech, discriminatory content, or content that is knowingly false or misleading.

15. Suspension & Termination Shopify Collabs — base, expanded

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, on written notice to the other party. SMTHG may also suspend or remove Influencer from the SMTHG Community, or terminate this Agreement immediately without notice, for fraud or fake engagement (Section 9), posting illegal or brand-unsafe content (Section 10), or violating the FTC disclosure requirement (Section 7). Sections 4 (Content License, for the remainder of its stated term), 7 (FTC Endorsement Disclosure, for posts made before termination), 12 (Confidentiality), 14 (IP Warranty), and 16 (Indemnification) survive termination.
