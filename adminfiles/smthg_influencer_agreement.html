This Agreement is between SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG"), and the individual or entity participating as an Influencer in the SMTHG Community ("Influencer").

1. Relationship Between the Parties SMTHG — added

SMTHG is a direct party to this Agreement. SMTHG pays Influencer compensation directly — Influencer is not paid by, and has no direct contractual relationship with, the Designer.

2. Independent Contractor Status Shopify Collabs — adapted

Influencer is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of SMTHG. Influencer is solely responsible for their own tax obligations, including self-employment tax, on any compensation received under this Agreement.

5. Content License Shopify Collabs — base

Influencer retains ownership of the content they create. Influencer grants SMTHG a perpetual, worldwide, transferable, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, display, and distribute that content for the purpose of operating and promoting SMTHG, including on the Website, the Community, and SMTHG's own social media accounts.

6. Virtual Try-On Images SMTHG — added

Where Influencer does not receive a physical sample of a Designer's product, Influencer may use SMTHG's Virtual Try-On (VTO) tool to generate images or video of the product for the content they publish. Content generated using VTO must accurately reflect the product's real color, fit, and materials as provided by SMTHG or the Designer, per the accuracy requirement in Section 7.

7. Product Claims & Designer Relationship SMTHG — added

Influencer may only describe a Designer's product using information provided as part of the collaboration (Section 3) or otherwise approved by SMTHG or the Designer, and must not make claims about price, availability, materials, or performance that are not accurate. Influencer is not authorized to make commitments, representations, or promises on behalf of any Designer or SMTHG, and is in agreement with SMTHG, not with any Designer (Section 1).

8. Third-Party Platforms Shopify Collabs — base

Influencer's use of any third-party platform (including Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube) to post content is governed by that platform's own terms. SMTHG is not responsible for the availability, performance, or policies of any third-party platform.

9. FTC Endorsement Disclosure SMTHG — added

Influencer must clearly and conspicuously disclose every sponsored or compensated post involving SMTHG or a Designer's products, every time, as required by the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). Where the platform provides a built-in disclosure tool (e.g. Instagram's Paid Partnership label, TikTok's Branded Content toggle), Influencer must use it in addition to an in-caption disclosure.

10. Compensation & Commission Structure SMTHG — added

SMTHG pays Influencer a commission on each Eligible Sale attributed to Influencer, under the following mechanism: Eligible Sale → Attribution (Influencer's link or code, as set out per collaboration in Section 3) → Commission ([insert %/tier]) → Refund/Chargeback Adjustment (Section 11) → Payout ([insert payout schedule]).

11. Refunds, Chargebacks & Fraud SMTHG — added

If a Sale attributed to Influencer is later cancelled, refunded, or successfully charged back, the Commission on that Sale is reversed — deducted from Influencer's next payout, or invoiced separately if no future payout is available to offset it. SMTHG may withhold or reverse Commission, and may immediately terminate this Agreement (Section 18), where SMTHG reasonably believes Influencer engaged in fraud, fake engagement, or artificially inflated sales.

12. Brand Safety SMTHG — added

Influencer will not associate SMTHG or a Designer's products with illegal content, sexually explicit content, hate speech, discriminatory content, or content that is knowingly false or misleading.

13. Exclusivity SMTHG — added

This Agreement does not restrict Influencer from working with other platforms alongside SMTHG.

14. Non-Circumvention SMTHG — added

For 1 year following the end of a collaboration facilitated by SMTHG, Influencer will not engage directly with the Designer involved in that collaboration — without SMTHG as intermediary — anywhere in the world, for product promotion, sponsorship, or sales arrangements of the kind covered by this Agreement.

18. Suspension & Termination Shopify Collabs — base, expanded

Sections 5 (Content License, for the remainder of its stated term), 9 (FTC Endorsement Disclosure, for posts made before termination), 14 (Non-Circumvention), 15 (Confidentiality), 17 (IP Warranty), and 19 (Indemnification) survive termination.
