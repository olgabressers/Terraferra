Influencer Relations
Relations
  • Find influencers by city and by brand; keep the Notion CRM boards up to date
  • Vet influencers: audience fit, follower quality, niche, and match with the style of each brand
  • Contact and remind influencers, run first calls, share the Founding Creator Guide
  • Explain the terms: 20% on every sale through their own link, no samples needed
  • Send the influencer agreement and get it signed
  • Handle commission queries and Founding Creator applications
  • Build long-term relationships with top performers for exclusive drops and co-marketing
Operations
  • Keep a page per influencer: profile, contact, style, call summary, contract details, next steps
  • Agree the format with each influencer: try-on content, live sessions with the brands, cross-posting
  • Brief influencers on upcoming drops: creative direction, timing, commission terms
  • Set up each influencer’s affiliate link to the drop
  • Track influencer GMV, conversion rate and engagement per drop; share performance reports
  • Influencer platform selection and payout management
  • Keep contact between influencers and brands through SMTHG
Year 1 budget, contractor$4,200/mo
US market$4,200–6,500/mo
US market source: Glassdoor / LinkedIn Salary 2025, Influencer / Creator Relations, fashion, US
Brand Relations
Relations
  • Find brands by city; keep the Notion brands CRM up to date
  • Recruit brands: first contact, the brand questionnaire, first calls, platform and try-on presentation
  • Explain the terms: commission on actual sales only, Founding Brand conditions, who ships and who handles returns
  • Send the brand agreement and get it signed
  • Run monthly brand success reviews: sell-through rates, engagement, recommendations
  • Own brand retention: identify at-risk brands, flag issues to the platform team
  • Maintain the long-term relationship with each brand, including a group chat per brand
Operations
  • Keep a page per brand: owner, contacts, stock, shipping, call summary, next steps
  • Agree the product selection for each drop with the brand
  • Send the content guide and collect content in one folder, with sizes for every product
  • Onboard the brand: profile setup, first drop walkthrough, rules briefing
  • Establish which system holds the brand’s stock and arrange the integration with the developers
  • Confirm shipping and returns: where stock sits, carrier, delivery time, duties, return handling
  • Test the brand page with the brand before launch
  • Ongoing brand support and payout management: payout every 45 days, after the return period

Customer Operations Manager
  • First contact for buyers, as SMTHG is the seller: resolve tickets through Intercom on orders, tracking, returns, payment disputes
  • Order logistics: pass orders to the brand, which ships direct, and follow each one to delivery
  • Cross-border shipments: follow carrier, customs and delivery issues with the brand
  • Returns: two weeks from receipt, handled with the brand; quality returns and chargebacks sit with the brand
  • Handle brand issues: dashboard access, drop submission errors
  • Maintain response times: under 4 hours first response, under 24 hours resolution; satisfaction tracking per brand
  • Build macros and FAQ for recurring queries
  • Document recurring quality complaints and fraud patterns; alert the platform team if systemic
