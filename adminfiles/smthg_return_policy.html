Return & Refund Policy

Applies to all items bought on smthg.us

1. Return window

You can return an item within 14 days after you receive it.

2. How to return

Start your return online on smthg.us. After your return is registered, we send you a return label with the return address.

The customer pays the cost of return shipping.

3. Return conditions

Only new and unworn items can be returned. Each item must be:

  • unworn, unwashed and unaltered;
  • returned with all original tags attached;
  • returned in its original packaging.

Items that do not meet these conditions are not refunded and are sent back to you.

4. Refunds

We refund you within 30 days after we receive your return. The refund goes to the payment method you used for the purchase. We send you an email when the refund is issued.

5. Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or is not the item you ordered, contact us within 14 days after you receive it, with your order number and a photo of the item.

6. Contact

Questions about returns: contact@smthg.us
