Privacy Policy

smthg.

This Privacy Policy explains how SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information through smthg. This Policy applies to Designers, Influencers, and Consumers who use the Services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and contact information — name, email address, phone number, mailing address.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment processor (Stripe); we do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Order information — products purchased, Drop participation, shipping and delivery details, returns and chargebacks.
  • Designer and Influencer information — business details, tax registration status, product listings, content submitted to smthg.
  • Usage information — pages visited, actions taken on the Services, device and browser information, collected automatically as described in Section 3.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Services — process orders, Drops, payouts, and returns.
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, and Services.
  • To send you marketing communications, where you have consented, and always with an option to opt out.
  • To maintain the security of the Services and prevent fraud.
  • To improve and develop the Services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

We use first-party strictly necessary cookies to operate the Services — these cannot be disabled, as they support core functions such as the cookie banner and remembering your settings.

We also use third-party analytics cookies to count visits, measure site performance, and understand which pages are most used. This information is aggregated and does not identify you individually.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share personal information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf, including payment processing (Stripe), cloud hosting, analytics, and communications tools.
  • Designers, limited to the order and shipping information needed to fulfil your purchase.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If this changes, we will update this Policy and provide the opt-out mechanism required by law before doing so.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, meet the purposes described in this Policy, or comply with our legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details in Section 9 and we will remove it.

7. Your Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you.
  • Request a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, or its use for targeted advertising (we do not currently engage in either — see Section 4).
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9. We will respond within 45 days, as required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, and store it on secure servers operated by us or our third-party service providers.

9. Contact Us

For any question about this Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at privacy@smthg.us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised Policy here with a new effective date.
