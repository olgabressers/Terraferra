SMTHG Community · Reference Cases
Circle Community Examples
Three live communities running on Circle — a media brand, a health coach, and a founder network. Each screenshot opens the live page.
Oprah Daily Insider
Media brand · paid membership
Open the membership page →
The model
All Access membership at $34.99/year (from $55). The members-only Oprah Insider Community is sold as one benefit in a bundle: weekly intention videos, "The Life You Want" classes, the magazine archive, a printed annual edition.
The community's role
"An inviting online space rooted in connection where you can share, learn and grow alongside like-minded Insiders" — daily prompts, weekly intentions, conversations around the brand's editorial topics.
Read for SMTHG
Community as part of a paid bundle around content and classes — the classroom + community + calendar set, under a strong consumer brand.
Buy Women Built
Founder network · brand movement
Open the community page →
The model
A UK movement of 2,000+ female founders of consumer brands. Membership costs "the price of two coffees" a month and includes all events plus the BWB Kitemark licence — a mark shown on member brands' products.
The community's role
Founders-only: advice channels by industry, local and virtual meet-ups, expert sessions, challenges. The app is Circle-built (app package: co.circle.buywomenbuilt) with the BWB brand on it.
Read for SMTHG
The closest analogue to the designer side: a paid, badge-bearing community of brand founders — peer advice, events, and a visibility mark that doubles purchase intent per buywomenbuilt.com.
Glucose Guide
Solo expert · free access + pay-what-you-can
Open the community →
The model
A diabetes coaching community where a lot is accessible free — including portions of the app: recipes, challenges, cooking classes, group coaching chat. Joining is free; subscriptions are pay-what-you-can ($5–50/month or $100 one-time), plus brand partnerships.
The community's role
The community is the product: spaces for daily check-ins, wins, news and events, courses and playbooks — delivered through its own branded mobile app on Circle.
Member onboarding — profile setup
New members enter through a profile-setup flow on the nutrition portal before reaching coaching: nutrition.glucoseguide.app/ProfileSetup →
Read for SMTHG
The free layer builds scale and habit; payment is optional and funds coaching. Daily check-in spaces are the come-back mechanic. Sources: Circle spotlight · Circle Discover.
The master example, translated to SMTHG
Section by section: how the Glucose Guide community is built, and what the same section becomes on SMTHG. Structure per Circle's community spotlight. First three sections — SMTHG side suggested · under research.
Glucose Guide · Section 1
New Member Hub — "Say hello 👋"
A start-here space plus a discussion area for introductions. A new member's first action is a post about themself — before any coaching, before any payment. The community answers, and the member has a reason to return the same day.
"A start here space, as well as a discussion area for introductions."

SMTHG · Section 1
Start Here — "Show your style"
The first action is a look, and words come second. A new member posts one photo of the outfit that feels most like them, plus three style tags. Designers introduce the brand with a video and its story; influencers introduce their audience. Every intro post feeds the style tribes and the tag library.
Pinned post — draft copyWelcome to SMTHG. Post one look that feels most like you — one photo, three style words. Your tags decide which drops and tribes we show you first.
Glucose Guide · Section 2
Health & Wellness Coaching
The expert layer: courses, playbooks, and a group coaching chat. Advice is delivered as community content — one coach answers where everyone reads, so each answer teaches many members at once. Member subscriptions fund this layer.
"Courses, playbooks, group coaching chat, and more."

SMTHG · Section 2
Style Coaching
The same expert layer with stylists in the coach's seat: styling courses for members, brand-building playbooks for designers, a creator school for influencers — and a group styling chat where one stylist's answer about fit, colour or occasion is read by the whole tribe. Fashion-psychology advice sits here as a service.
Space description — draft copyAsk a stylist anything: what fits, what matches, what to wear where. One question, answered for everyone.
Glucose Guide · Section 3
Recipes
A library of recipes, from daily meals to tactical glucose advice. The recipe is the community's unit of value: small, useful, endlessly shareable — and each one keeps working long after it is posted. 30 new recipes a month with meal plans for paying members.
"A library of recipes, ranging from daily meals to tactical glucose advice."

SMTHG · Section 3
Looks Library
The recipe becomes the look. A library of outfit formulas — daily looks, occasion looks, one-piece-three-ways — each built from drop pieces and a member's own wardrobe. Looks carry the style names members coin, and the library doubles as the discovery vocabulary of the platform. New looks arrive with every drop.
Entry format — draft copyThe look: [style name]. Built from: [drop piece] + what you own. Worn by: [member]. Save it, wear it, name your own version.
Growth dynamics — smthg.us ↔ community
How consumers move between the shop and the community, and how each loop turns into sales. Pattern observed in the Glucose Guide community (logged-in view) and translated to SMTHG. All dynamics suggested · under research.
#DynamicHow it moves peopleHow it becomes salesSource
1 Tool first Virtual try-on and the wardrobe tool are free inside the community — the useful thing one tab from the feed, like Carb Counter in Glucose Guide's nav. Try the look on your own photo before any purchase; the result posts to the tribe. Every tried-on piece links to its live drop on smthg.us. The tool visit starts before intent; checkout is one click after it. Deck v18, slide 6 · GG nav, observed
2 Visible padlock Early-access and next-drop preview spaces sit in every member's sidebar with a 🔒 — like GG's Subscriber Bonuses. The padlock opens by level (points) or by owning a previous drop. The padlock sells position: 24h early access to a window that closes. Participation and purchases are the two keys. GG sidebar, observed · Skool levels
3 Daily ritual Today's-look check-in with unread badges — the reason to open today, and to open now. GG runs the same loop with the Daily Blood Sugar Check-in. Every posted look carries its shoppable pieces; the daily ritual doubles as a daily shop window between drops. GG check-in [2] badge, observed · Referral §04
4 Drop as live event The drop runs as an event inside the community — countdown watched together, live stock counter, "join 47 others" — like GG's summit with its own chat and speakers. The thread feeds checkout on smthg.us in the moment of highest urgency; the sold-out card posts back and warms the next drop. GG summit spaces, observed · Referral §04
5 Owner looks & wins Sold-out posts, owner cards and member looks — GG's Product Recs and Share-your-wins, translated. Buying grants community identity: the owner badge and the designer's circle. Every win and owner look links to that designer's next-drop waitlist. Yesterday's buyer recruits tomorrow's. GG spaces, observed · Referral §04
6 Calendar & waitlists in the sidebar The drop calendar and waitlists live where the member already is daily — like GG's Update Membership link set. Drops land at set moments, the Breeze 19:00 rhythm. Commerce intent is parked one click away every day; the waitlist converts the moment the window opens. GG Links, observed · breeze.social
7 Shareable proof outward What leaves the platform: sold-out cards, owner badges, super-fan try-on videos. Each lands outside with a link to the tribe and the waitlist. One share recruits a community member and a future buyer at once — the closed pain produces the share, the share lands on smthg.us. Close the Pain · Gamification §01
8 One account, two surfaces Every smthg.us product page links into its drop thread; every community post showing clothing carries "the pieces in this look" back to smthg.us. Single sign-on both ways. No seam between reading and buying — the member never chooses between the community and the shop. Circle Plus SSO, circle.so/pricing
