1. Definitions Designer T&C — base

"Website" means the online store SMTHG operates under the smthg. name. "Products" means the goods Brand supplies for sale on the Website. "Customer" means a buyer on the Website. "Order" means a Customer's paid order for Products. "Retail Price" means the price a Customer pays for a Product, excluding sales tax. "GMV" means the total Retail Price of Orders, less refunds. "Purchase Price" means the Retail Price less the Platform Commission. "Fees" means the Onboarding Fee, Platform Fee and Platform Commission in Section 3. "Influencer" means a person who promotes Products under an agreement with SMTHG; "Creator" means the same.

2. SMTHG as Seller of Record, Brand as Supplier smthg. — added

2.1 Seller of Record. SMTHG is the seller of record for every sale on the Website. SMTHG sells the Products to Customers in its own name and collects payment from them.

2.2 Brand as supplier. Brand supplies each sold Product to SMTHG at the Purchase Price and fulfils the Order directly to the Customer under Section 5. Title to a Product passes to SMTHG when the Customer pays and to the Customer on delivery. Risk of loss or damage stays with Brand until the Customer receives the Product.

2.3 Resale authorization. Brand authorizes SMTHG to resell the Products and to use their photos, descriptions and trademarks to promote them, as set out in Section 11.

Brand does not sign the terms of SMTHG's technology providers, such as Shopify. This Agreement is the whole contract between Brand and SMTHG.

4. Listings, Prices and Stock Designer T&C — base PuppetVendors — added

Brand gives SMTHG accurate and complete Product information: description, materials and fibre content, country of origin, care instructions, sizes and images, and keeps it current. SMTHG decides which Products appear on the Website and may edit or remove any listing.

Brand sets the Retail Price after consulting SMTHG. The Retail Price includes shipping to the Customer. Brand does not sell the same Product for a lower price on its own website or any other channel.

Brand keeps stock levels accurate in the stock connection SMTHG uses and updates them within one business day of any change. Brand fulfils every Order placed for a Product shown as in stock. 6. Returns, Refunds and Defects Designer T&C — base

SMTHG is responsible to the Customer for returns and refunds, sets the Customer return policy on the Website and deals with Customers. Brand performs the return and defect obligations in this Section. Brand accepts returned Products at its return address and confirms their condition within 5 business days of receipt. When SMTHG refunds a Customer, the Purchase Price of that Product is deducted from Brand's next payment. Brand bears all costs of a defective, wrong, damaged or not-as-described Product, including shipping both ways and the replacement or refund.

11. Brand Content and Virtual Try-On Designer T&C — base smthg. — added

Brand keeps ownership of its trademarks, designs, images and other content. Brand grants SMTHG a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license, which SMTHG may sublicense to its service providers and Influencers, to use, copy, edit, crop, translate and display Brand's Product images, videos, descriptions, names, logos and trademarks to list, advertise and promote the Products and smthg. on the Website, social media, paid advertising, email and the smthg. community. This includes using Product images to create Virtual Try-On images with artificial intelligence. Brand warrants that its content does not infringe anyone's rights.

The license lasts while this Agreement is in force. After it ends, SMTHG removes the listings within 14 days but does not have to delete posts, advertisements and Influencer content already published.

Photos, videos, Virtual Try-On images and other content made by SMTHG or Influencers do not belong to Brand. Brand may use Influencer content about its Products on its own channels to the extent SMTHG's Influencer Agreement allows, crediting the Influencer and following the US Federal Trade Commission's endorsement rules.

13. Customer Data Designer T&C — base

Customer data belongs to SMTHG. Brand uses the Customer data it receives only to ship and handle the Order, keeps it secure, and deletes it 30 days after the return period for that Order ends. Brand tells SMTHG within 72 hours of any loss of or unauthorised access to Customer data.

14. Confidentiality ShopMy — added

All non-public information Brand receives about SMTHG, its Influencers, Customers or campaigns, including commission rates, sales data and this Agreement's terms, is confidential. Brand does not disclose or use it except to perform this Agreement. SMTHG keeps Brand's non-public information confidential in the same way. This Section survives the end of this Agreement.

15. Term, Suspension and Termination Designer T&C — base

This Agreement continues until either party ends it on 30 days' notice by email. SMTHG may suspend a listing or Brand's account at any time, and may end this Agreement immediately by email if Brand breaches it, sells counterfeit or unsafe Products, acts fraudulently, becomes insolvent, becomes subject to sanctions under Section 20, or exposes SMTHG to legal or reputational harm.

When this Agreement ends: Brand ships every Order placed before the end date and handles its returns; SMTHG pays the amounts due under Section 8 after its holds and deductions; Fees already paid are not refunded; and SMTHG owes Brand no compensation for ending the Agreement.
