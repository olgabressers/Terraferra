1. Definitions

Drop
A fixed-window, pre-order sale event for your products on smthg.
GMV
The gross value of all confirmed pre-orders in a Drop, before any Fees.
Platform Commission
10% of GMV, payable to SMTHG on every sale.
Influencer Program Fee
10% (standard) or 20% (Founding Creator tier) of GMV attributed to influencer involvement in a Drop, paid out to the influencer.
Platform Fee
$500 per month.
Onboarding Fee
$250, one-time, at sign-up.
Statement
The monthly statement of Drop sales, Fees, and amount due, available on your Designer Dashboard.
Customer
A buyer on smthg.
