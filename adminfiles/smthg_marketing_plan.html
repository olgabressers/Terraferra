02One message per audience
BRANDS
Find the people who’ll love what you make.
  • Creators sell your drop. You make it.
  • Brands of a similar style share their buyers.
CREATORS
Turn your taste into discovery.
  • Wear new designers through try-on, no samples.
  • Earn on what your audience buys.
SHOPPERS
Find your next favorite. Make it your own.
  • Try it on yourself before you buy.
  • Follow the brands, share your looks.

04Every drop, in four steps
BEFORE
Build the waitlist
  • Creators get try-on looks of the pieces.
  • Brand Space opens; teasers on Instagram and email.
OPEN
Drop day
  • Creators post with their links.
  • Alert to the email list and members.
LIVE
Drop window
  • Live session with the brand in the community.
  • Reposts of creator and buyer looks.
AFTER
Next drop starts bigger
  • Buyers join the Brand Space.
  • Those who missed it go first on the next drop.
05Who does what
SMTHG
We run
  • Creator selection, brief and campaign.
  • Community, Instagram, email, ads.
  • Store, payment, tax, results report.
BRAND
The brand gives
  • Photos, story and stock for the drop.
  • Live session in its Brand Space.
  • Shipping and returns.
06What we measure after each drop
Per drop
  • Sales and sales per creator.
  • Try-ons and new members.
  • Email sign-ups and live event attendance.
07To decide on Wednesday
Open decisions
  • Ad budget for the pilot, and who pays.
  • Fashion week: which brands, which creators, who is there.
  • Date and format of the live event.
  • Owner for each channel.
  • Creator attribution window: 30 or 45 days.
