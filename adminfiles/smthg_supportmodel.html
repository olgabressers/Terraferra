Activities & Volume · MVP

Influencers

Activities
  • Recruiting / outreach
  • Vetting — engagement rate, audience fit
  • Drop briefing
  • Content / UGC review
  • Commission & payout queries
  • Founding Creator tier applications
  • Referral-share support (Instagram/Facebook invite loop)
MVP volume
15–50 active, rising
5 influencers/drop default (MVP Costs calculator) × 3–10 drops/mo, tracking the designer ramp below. Outreach messages: small-batch, tens/mo at pilot — not the 200–300/day full-recruiting rate.

Designers

Activities
  • Recruitment / outreach (B2B)
  • Contract signing ($1,000/mo services agreement)
  • Onboarding — profile, catalog upload, first drop walkthrough
  • Drop submission errors / stock sync
  • Payout & commission-clawback queries
  • So-Me-Space curation
  • Retention check-ins
MVP volume
3–10 designers, rising
3–5 at pilot launch (Operations Team Day-1 trigger) → 10 by Month 6–9 (GTM roadmap). 1 drop/designer/mo → 3–10 onboarding/support/payout touches/mo per category, scaling with cohort.

Consumers

Activities
  • Order status / tracking
  • Returns & exchanges
  • Payment disputes
  • Platform how-to — drops, pre-order, VTO, sizing
  • Account / login issues
  • Referral program questions
  • Missed-drop / notification complaints
  • General pre-launch inquiries (waitlist, press) — unquantified
MVP volume
~6–25 tickets/mo, rising
75–250 orders/mo (3–10 designers × 1 drop × ~25 orders/drop, MVP Costs calculator default) × 4–9 tickets/100 orders (Zendesk Ecomm CX) ≈ 3–23/mo. Excludes unquantified pre-launch inquiries above.

Recommendation

Support model

One combined part-time contractor, hired now — not CEO-covered, not three separate day-1 hires, not outsourced BPO for influencers/designers.

Cost: ~$1,500/mo — already inside the Year 1 offshore-contractor rate in Budget, brought forward and consolidated into one role. Consumer support layers Intercom + Influx overflow on top — doesn't require this hire to answer every ticket personally.

Split triggerAction
Influencer outreach becomes daily/systematic, or 25+ active influencersDedicated Influencer Relations hire
Designer count needs real curationDedicated Community Manager hire
Consumer tickets ≥ 150/moInflux upgrade to dedicated agent ($2,100/mo) or dedicated part-time CS hire

Sources: Operations Team (pilot designer trigger, CS benchmarks), GTM (10 designers by Month 6–9), MVP Costs (drop economics, Shopify/PuppetVendors, Insense/Collabstr), Operating Budget (Intercom, Influx, HubSpot, Modash, Archive — Terra Ferra-branded working budget; costs cross-checked against active Budget), Architecture (Stripe Connect, Instagram/TikTok API), Zendesk Ecomm CX Report (tickets per 100 orders).
