smthg. Support Model

Activities, MVP volume, staffing model, and technology per operational pillar.

One combined internal contractor covers all three pillars at MVP — reactive consumer tickets stay low (order volume is small pre-launch), but designer onboarding and influencer recruiting are relationship-heavy from day one regardless of GMV. No new tech spend required: Intercom and Stripe Connect are already budgeted; everything else is a free shared tracker until a split trigger fires.

Active count: 5 influencers/drop default (MVP Costs calculator) × 3–5 pilot drops/mo. Outreach volume: small-batch testing per pilot approach — tens/mo, not the 200–300/day full-recruiting rate, until outreach goes systematic (split trigger below).

Pilot cohort size = Day-1 hire trigger (Operations Team). 1 drop/designer/mo → onboarding + drop-support + payout touch per designer per month.

Consumers

Activities
  • Order status / tracking FAQ
  • Returns FAQ
  • Payment disputes Escalation
  • Platform how-to (drops, VTO, sizing) FAQ
MVP volume
~6–12 tickets/mo
$0–50K GMV / 6mo ÷ $200 AOV ≈ 42 orders/mo avg × 4–9 tickets/100 orders (Zendesk Ecomm CX) ≈ 2–4/mo. Doesn't include pre-launch general inquiries (waitlist, "how does this work") — no sourced benchmark exists for that volume; treat as unquantified addition, not zero.

04 Recommendation

Support model

One combined part-time contractor, hired now — not CEO-covered, not three separate day-1 hires, not outsourced BPO.
