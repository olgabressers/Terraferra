Recommendation

Support model

One combined part-time contractor, hired now — not CEO-covered, not three separate day-1 hires, not outsourced BPO.

Cost: ~$1,500/mo — already inside the Year 1 offshore-contractor rate in Budget, brought forward and consolidated into one role.

Split triggerAction
Consumer tickets ≥ 150/moDedicated part-time CS hire
Influencer outreach becomes daily/systematic, or 25+ active influencersDedicated Influencer Relations hire
Designer count needs real curationDedicated Community Manager hire
Chatdesk — rejected for now. Supported integrations are Zendesk, Gorgias, Front, Freshdesk, Shopify, Magento — Intercom (SMTHG's chosen tool) is not among them. Requires historical ticket data to build its response guide; SMTHG has none pre-launch. Pricing only competitive past ~2,000 tickets/mo — beyond Year 1 volume.

Grok Bot — not needed. Same draft-and-approve function Intercom Fin AI already provides natively on the tool already in the stack. No separate purchase.

Sources: Operations Team (role triggers, CS benchmarks), MVP Costs (drop economics, AOV), Architecture (Intercom, Stripe Connect stack), Budget (Year 1 contractor rate). Consumer/influencer/designer support tooling evaluation: Chatdesk (chatdesk.com) and Grok Bot (x.ai) reviewed and rejected — see internal chat log.
