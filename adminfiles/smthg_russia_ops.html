01Customs & Compliance — by Route
RU, EU, and UK have no US free trade agreement — same MFN tariff treatment for all three. Below $800 goods value, all clear automatically with zero formal customs entry.

03Order Flow
1
Customer pays on the platform. Stripe collects payment + US sales tax (Stripe Tax, automatic).
2
SMTHG notifies the brand of the order.
3
Brand packs the item and books DHL.
4
DHL generates the invoice, packing list, and waybill, and clears US customs under the $800 de minimis.
5
Brand ships direct to the customer. SMTHG never touches the goods.
6
Stripe splits the payment — brand's share to their non-Russian bank account (Georgian, Armenian, or EU), SMTHG keeps commission.
