Russia, EU, UK, and Turkey → US and EU. Research current as of August 2026 — re-verify carrier status before a live launch.
Verdict
Works on the platform, but not paperwork-free for any origin. Section 321 de minimis is suspended for all countries — every US-bound shipment now needs a customs entry, a broker, and a bond. Turkey→EU is the one clean exception (0% duty, one certificate).

Applies to every US-bound route (RU, EU, UK, Turkey→US). Turkey→EU does not need this — no US customs involved.

DHL, FedEx, or UPS The carrier — all three are licensed US customs brokers $0 — covered by the carrier's own bond Folded into the carrier's clearance charge (confirmed for RU→US DHL: $540–720 total; not confirmed for EU/UK/Turkey) Nobody — automatic

07Order Flow
Worked example using Russia → US — same flow for every origin, only the carrier and payout account change.
1
Customer pays on the platform. Stripe collects payment + US sales tax (Stripe Tax, automatic).
2
SMTHG notifies the brand of the order.
3
Brand packs the item and books DHL.
4
DHL generates the invoice, packing list, and waybill, and files the customs entry (broker included, §02).
5
Brand ships direct to the customer. SMTHG never touches the goods.
6
Stripe splits the payment — brand's share to their payout account (non-Russian account required for Russia-based brands), SMTHG keeps commission.
