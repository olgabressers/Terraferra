Code of Ethics

smthg.

This Code of Ethics describes how we ask everyone on smthg. to treat each other and do business — Designers, Influencers, and SMTHG itself. We built it because we care about who we work with, not just what they sell, and we expect the same care in return. A breach of this Code is grounds for suspension or termination under the applicable Terms & Conditions.

1. What We Ask of Everyone

We ask everyone on smthg. to be honest about who they are, what they're selling, and how they represent it — and to comply with the law that applies to them. Counterfeit goods and infringement of anyone else's intellectual property have no place on the platform. We expect respectful conduct from everyone, with no harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior toward anyone else on smthg. We don't tolerate bribes, kickbacks, or other improper payments used to gain an advantage on the platform, and we ask everyone to protect any non-public information they access through it, including other people's data.

2. What We Ask of Designers

We work with independent designers we're proud to stand behind, and we ask a few things in return.

Fair labor. Everyone in your supply chain must work in a safe environment, be paid at least the applicable minimum wage, and work reasonable hours — and your supply chain must be free of trafficked, bonded, forced, or child labor, at every step.

Inclusive brands. We work with brands that welcome and represent people of all backgrounds, and we expect the same commitment to inclusion from every designer we feature.

Animal welfare. We don't accept farmed fur, exotic skins, or feathers. Hides are only acceptable when they're a co-product of the meat industry — never from an animal killed for its skin alone.

Sustainable materials. If you work with endangered or exotic hardwoods, use off-cuts or recycled wood, and never source from illegal logging or deforestation.

Claims you can back up. Before you make a claim like "sustainable," "ethical," "handmade," or "eco-friendly," you need competent and reliable evidence for it in hand — not after the fact. Broad, unqualified claims are exactly what the FTC's Green Guides (16 CFR Part 260) warn against: say what you actually mean, e.g. "made from 80% recycled cotton" rather than just "sustainable." An unsupported claim is an unfair or deceptive act or practice under Section 5 of the FTC Act (15 U.S.C. § 45).

Product safety. Comply with applicable US product-safety and labeling law, as set out further in the Designer Terms & Conditions.

3. What We Ask of Influencers

We're glad to have you telling our designers' stories, and we ask you to do it honestly.

Disclose paid relationships. Clearly and conspicuously disclose every sponsored or compensated post, every time, as required by the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255).

Speak from experience. Only make claims about a product you've actually used, and never fabricate results.

Respectful community conduct. No harassment, hate speech, or misleading engagement tactics like bots, fake followers, or fake reviews.

4. What We Ask of Ourselves

We hold SMTHG to the same standard we hold everyone else to.

Equal treatment. We don't discriminate in hiring, curation, or any platform decision based on a protected characteristic, and we choose to work with inclusive companies as a matter of policy, not preference.

Conflicts of interest. We disclose any personal or financial interest that could influence a platform decision.

Data privacy. We handle personal information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Anti-bribery. We don't offer or accept bribes, including in dealings with any party outside the US, consistent with the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.

No retaliation. We won't retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith under Section 5.

5. Reporting a Concern

If you believe this Code has been violated, tell us at contact@smthg.us. We review every report and act on it where appropriate, under the applicable Terms & Conditions.

6. Enforcement

A violation of this Code may result in suspension or termination of a Designer's, Influencer's, or team member's relationship with SMTHG, as set out in the applicable Terms & Conditions.
