Code of Ethics

smthg.

This Code of Ethics sets the standards of conduct for everyone on smthg. — Designers, Influencers, and SMTHG itself. Breach of this Code is grounds for suspension or termination under the applicable Terms & Conditions.

1. Core Principles — Everyone

  • Honesty and legal compliance. Represent yourself, your products, and your claims accurately, and comply with applicable law.
  • No counterfeits, no infringement. Never list, promote, or sell counterfeit goods or infringe another party's intellectual property.
  • Respectful conduct. No harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior toward anyone on the platform.
  • No bribery or corruption. No bribes, kickbacks, or improper payments to gain an advantage on the platform.
  • Confidentiality. Protect any non-public information you access through the platform, including other users' data.

2. For Designers

  • Fair labor. No sweatshop, trafficked, bonded, child, or forced labor anywhere in your supply chain.
  • Animal welfare. No farmed fur, exotic skins, or feathers. Hides are only acceptable as a co-product of the meat industry — never from an animal killed for its skin alone.
  • Sustainable materials. If working with endangered or exotic hardwoods, use off-cuts or recycled wood — never source from illegal logging or deforestation.
  • Substantiated claims. Any "sustainable," "ethical," "handmade," or similar claim must be true and substantiated — unsupported claims are a deceptive trade practice under the FTC Act and the FTC's Green Guides.
  • Product safety. Comply with applicable US product-safety and labeling law, as set out further in the Designer Terms & Conditions.

3. For Influencers

  • Disclose paid relationships. Clearly and conspicuously disclose any sponsored or compensated content, as required by the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) — every sponsored post, every time.
  • Authentic representation. Only make claims about a product you have actually used or that are true, and never fabricate results.
  • Respectful community conduct. No harassment, hate speech, or misleading engagement tactics (bots, fake followers, fake reviews).

4. For SMTHG

  • Equal treatment. No discrimination in hiring, curation, or platform decisions based on a protected characteristic.
  • Conflicts of interest. Disclose any personal or financial interest that could influence a platform decision.
  • Data privacy. Handle personal information as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Anti-bribery. No offering or accepting of bribes, including in dealings with any party outside the US, consistent with the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.
  • No retaliation. No retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith under Section 5.

5. Reporting a Concern

If you believe this Code has been violated, contact us at ethics@smthg.us. Reports are reviewed and, where appropriate, acted on under the applicable Terms & Conditions.

6. Enforcement

A violation of this Code may result in suspension or termination of a Designer's, Influencer's, or team member's relationship with SMTHG, as set out in the applicable Terms & Conditions.
