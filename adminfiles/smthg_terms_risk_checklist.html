A. General / Platform-Wide Documents 6 documents (2 chapters nested under Privacy Policy)

Apply across all three roles (Designers, Influencers, Consumers) — not specific to one contract. 6 documents, each its own row: Privacy Policy, Code of Ethics, DMCA/Copyright Policy, Community Guidelines, Cookie Policy, Accessibility Statement. CCPA and CAN-SPAM are indented "↳" rows directly under Privacy Policy — they're chapters inside it, not separate documents.
