01Brand and owner

  • Founded in 2015. Production in Ukraine.
  • Pyjama-style suits. Animal prints on beige and white backgrounds.
  • Focus on the US market.
  • Took part in New York Fashion Week and works with a showroom.
  • Katerina's focus is sales: own showroom or collaborations. The brand does a lot of marketing and shows.
  • Partner: Alexander. He joined the call of 28 October and takes questions as they come up.

02Sales and returns

  • Sales go to loyal clients, through shops, the showroom and pop-ups.
  • Online sales are not active, so returns are very limited. Returns from shops, the showroom and pop-ups are also very limited.
  • Price level: $250–300.

03Production and lead times

  • Capsules of 70 items in total.
  • Sizes: XS-S-M as one size, L-XL as the second.
  • Largest batch: 15 items; also batches of 30 items.
  • Production lead time: up to 1.5 months.

04Call 28 October 2026 – summary

  • Participants: Katerina and Alexander (La Magnetique), SMTHG.
  • Product for the test: Relaxed fit satin set with nature-inspired print, one of the brand's most popular models. Two colourways: white and beige (nude).
  • Unisex: the collection is not split into men's and women's. Men's orders are sent in L-XL.
  • There is no time to sew new pieces before the launch, so the drop uses stock already in the US. Some positions have only 1–3 units left. Single units do not work for the drop, because creators earn a percentage per sale.
  • Exact quantity is not known yet: a parcel was delivered by the logistics company to the wrong person in another state.
  • Alexander proposed to focus on the two popular colourways, beige and white, so orders do not come in for items that are not in stock.
  • Price: $299 instead of the $249 the suit sold for before, proposed by Alexander. In his view $299 is the right level for this quality and concept compared with competitors. The price on the brand's own website changes to $299, so both prices match.
  • Alexander asked whether SMTHG shows the price and taxes separately. Answer: yes, taxes are calculated per state at checkout. He noted shipping can cost from $10 to $50 depending on the destination.
  • US shipping is included in the price. Shipping is to the US only. Taxes are calculated separately per state at checkout.
  • SMTHG works without physical samples: creators use virtual try-on only. SMTHG sends a demo link where Katerina can upload herself and the clothes.
  • Launch target: 14 November 2026, during fashion week, about 2.5 weeks from the call.
