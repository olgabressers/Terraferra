Florida Men’s Fashion Week × smthg

Virtual try-on at the show: the process.

  • Guests try on the collections from the show with Virtual Try-On, right at the event.
  • The aim is presence and contact with the fashion audience: light, native PR for smthg. at low cost.
  • Brands get a try-on feature for their collection, given to them free by the organisers.
  • One QR code and one page for the whole event. Logos and design are prepared now; collection photos can go in on the last day.
