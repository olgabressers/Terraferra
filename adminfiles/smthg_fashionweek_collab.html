Virtual try-on at the show

For visitors

Visitors try on clothes from the brands at the show and keep the photo.

For brands

Visitors try on pieces from the brand’s previous collections. Each piece is labelled with the brand name.

For the fashion week

A try-on for your visitors, with the fashion week logo at the top of the page.

For smthg.

We are at the show and meet the fashion week audience.

Phase 1Before the show

1

Designers

Fashion week team

Send us the list of designers taking part.

2

Collection photos

Designers

Send photos of pieces from their previous shows. Photos can be added until the last day.

3

Event page

smthg

We make one page with all brands and their pieces, with the smthg. and fashion week logos at the top.

4

QR code

smthg · Fashion week team

We make one QR code that opens the event page. It is printed by the fashion week team, or we print it and send it to the venue. We need the size, the number of copies and the delivery address.

5

Placement

Fashion week team · smthg

QR codes go at the mirror, the photo zone or the entrance.

Phase 2At the show

6

Scan

Visitors scan the QR code.

7

Choose

They see all brands on one page and pick a piece.

8

Add a photo

They upload a photo or take a selfie, for example in the mirror.

9

Try it on

They see the piece on themselves. While the photo loads, they get a compliment and an invitation: “Become our model”.

10

Save and share

They save the photo and post it in their stories, tagging the brand, the fashion week and smthg.

Phase 3After the show

11

Repost

Brands · fashion week · smthg

Brands, the fashion week and smthg. repost the stories they are tagged in.
