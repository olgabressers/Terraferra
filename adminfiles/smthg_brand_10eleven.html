01Call 2 November 2026 – summary

  • Participants: Arina (10eleven), SMTHG.
  • Arina works with the brand since it started: she launched the Instagram and the targeted ads. The brand has released one drop, a very basic one. The second drop has more of the brand's identity and more unique pieces.
  • 10eleven needs influencers and media for the second drop and wants to build a community. Arina's questions: how SMTHG works with influencers and media, and how payment works.
  • Arina could not answer how many items each product had in the first drop. The operations director handles it, and she will request the information.
  • Production and shipping to Miami: Arina said she is not fully competent on this.
  • Tax and delivery outside the US: for some countries, such as Canada and Greece, the tax a customer pays on delivery is higher than the price of the item. 10eleven has not solved this yet.
  • Content: the brand's content stays the brand's. Influencer content goes to SMTHG's platform, where the product can be bought. The brand can repost it.

02Terms and conditions presented to Arina

  • Commission: 30% of sales. Nothing is paid if nothing sells.
  • Founding Brand: no onboarding fee and no $500 monthly fee for the first 12 months. The contract is in preparation and will state this.
  • The pilot runs in the US only. Prices on SMTHG include all costs, including delivery within the US. The price should match the brand's website.
  • Taxes are calculated by Shopify by state.
  • Arina's question: whether the first month is a trial period. The answer given: the time does not matter, the drop has to sell.
  • No exclusive pieces are required at the start.
  • Virtual try-on replaces physical samples. SMTHG covers the cost of each generation.
  • The brand's effort: choose products and send photos, sizes and descriptions, or the link to the product pages. Video is optional.

03Product and timing discussed

  • SMTHG: at least 20–25 pieces per model, an easy size range, a simple product to keep returns low, and an original element rather than a plain white T-shirt. Pants without stretch are not recommended.
  • SMTHG suggested a hoodie and two T-shirts from the current stock.
  • SMTHG: do not wait for the mid-December drop. Sales slow down after 15 December and the Christmas gifting period is the best moment to launch.
  • Arina: the second drop date came from the brand's management. She prefers to test with the second drop and to enter step by step with what already exists. She will bring SMTHG's proposal to the decision.

04Next steps

  • Arina: brief the operations director on 3 November, in the evening. If he decides it needs sign-off from the top of the company, it goes there. Otherwise 10eleven moves forward on its own. She will write back to SMTHG.
  • Arina: request from the operations director the number of items per model.
  • SMTHG: send Arina on Telegram the SMTHG website link, the virtual try-on link and the brand onboarding guide.
