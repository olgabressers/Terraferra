04Call 2 November 2026 – summary

  • Participants: Arina (10eleven), SMTHG.
  • 10eleven needs influencers and media for the second drop and wants to build a community. Arina's questions: how SMTHG works with influencers and media, and how payment works.
  • Terms presented: 30% commission on sales, nothing paid if nothing sells. Founding Brand: no onboarding fee and no $500 monthly fee for the first 12 months. The contract is in preparation and will state this.
  • The pilot runs in the US only. Prices on SMTHG include all costs, including delivery within the US, and should match the brand's website. Shopify calculates taxes by state.
  • Arina asked whether the first month is a trial period. Answer given: the time does not matter, the drop has to sell.
  • No exclusive pieces are required at the start. Virtual try-on replaces physical samples; SMTHG covers the cost of each generation.
  • What the brand does: choose products and send photos, sizes and descriptions, or the link to the product pages. Video is optional.
  • Content: the brand's content stays the brand's. Influencer content goes to SMTHG's platform, where the product can be bought. The brand can repost it.
  • SMTHG advice on product: at least 20–25 pieces per model, an easy size range, a simple product to keep returns low, and an original element rather than a plain white T-shirt. Pants without stretch are not recommended. SMTHG suggested a hoodie and two T-shirts from the current stock.
  • SMTHG advice on timing: do not wait for the mid-December drop. Sales slow down after 15 December and the Christmas gifting period is the best moment to launch.
  • Arina: the second drop date came from the brand's management. She prefers to test with the second drop and to enter step by step with what already exists. She will bring SMTHG's proposal to the decision.
