Privacy Policy — Draft v2

smthg.

Draft candidate — not published as the site's Privacy Policy

SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG", "we", "us", or "our"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit smthg.us (the "Website") or subscribe for SMTHG updates.

This Policy applies to the Website only. It does not apply to a SMTHG shop hosted on a separate domain or to the SMTHG community hosted on Circle; those services will display their own privacy information.

Please also read our Cookie Policy, which explains how we use cookies and similar technologies.

1. Personal Information We Collect

Information you provide

If you subscribe for updates, we collect the email address you submit. We use this information to send you SMTHG news, product updates, and early-drop communications if you choose to receive them.

Providing your email address is optional. However, we cannot send you these updates without it.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Website, we and our service providers may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, the date and time of your visit, and security or diagnostic logs.

If you consent to Performance Cookies, we may also collect online identifiers and information about your use of the Website through Google Analytics 4 and/or PostHog. Details of these technologies and the choices available to you are in our Cookie Policy.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

  • operate, secure, and improve the Website;
  • process and manage your subscription for SMTHG updates;
  • send marketing emails only where you have chosen to receive them;
  • understand Website use through optional analytics, where you have consented;
  • respond to questions or requests you send us; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our rights.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar law applies, our legal bases are:

  • Consent for marketing emails and optional Performance Cookies;
  • Legitimate interests for Website security, fraud prevention, technical maintenance, and improving the Website in ways that do not override your rights; and
  • Legal obligations where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

You may withdraw consent to marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in any such email. You may withdraw consent to optional cookies through Cookie Settings in the Website footer. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

3. How We Share Personal Information

We share personal information only as needed for the purposes above, including with:

  • Framer, which hosts the Website and supports its forms and technical operation;
  • Google Analytics 4 and PostHog, only if you consent to the relevant analytics technologies and they are enabled on the Website;
  • Circle, which we use to manage and send SMTHG email communications to subscribers; and
  • legal, regulatory, or professional advisers and authorities when required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users, or the Website.

We do not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. International Transfers

SMTHG and some of our service providers may process personal information outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where a transfer is subject to applicable data-protection law, we use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. You may contact us to request more information about these safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • subscription information is retained while you remain subscribed; after you unsubscribe, we may retain limited information needed to honour your opt-out and demonstrate compliance;
  • analytics information is retained according to the settings of the relevant analytics provider and only for as long as reasonably necessary to understand and improve the Website; and
  • technical and security logs are retained for the period reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to our processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to data portability and to withdraw consent.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

If a U.S. state privacy law applies to you, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of personal information, and to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising where applicable. We do not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise a privacy right, email contact@smthg.us. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice where required by law.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at contact@smthg.us.

Comparison — v2 Draft vs. Live v1

Same substance: no-sale/no-cross-context-ad-sharing commitment, children's privacy, contact email, right to update the Policy.

Scope — this is the biggest structural difference

  • v1 is written for the full live marketplace — it covers Designers, Influencers, and Consumers, and lists marketplace data categories (payment via Stripe, orders, Drop participation, Designer/Influencer business details).
  • v2 is scoped to the pre-launch marketing website only — email subscription plus automatically-collected technical/analytics data. It explicitly carves out a separate shop domain and a Circle-hosted community as having their own privacy notices. No orders, payments, or Designer/Influencer data appear anywhere in v2 — those apply once the actual marketplace goes live, not to the current pre-launch site.

Jurisdiction — same tension as the Cookie Policy comparison

  • v1 covers US state privacy laws only (CA, VA, CO, CT, and similar).
  • v2 adds a full GDPR/UK GDPR legal-bases section (Consent / Legitimate Interests / Legal Obligations), an International Transfers section referencing the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses, and an EEA/UK/Switzerland right to complain to a data-protection authority.
  • Nothing elsewhere in the project documents an EU/UK launch — if this draft reflects an actual decision to serve EU/UK users (or is simply future-proofing before any US-vs-EU decision is made), that should be confirmed, since it's the same open question flagged on the Cookie Policy draft.

Structure

  • v1 has a standalone Cookies section (its Section 3) that duplicates some Cookie Policy content. v2 removes that duplication entirely and just cross-references the separate Cookie Policy — cleaner, matches how the site's own Cookie Policy v1/v2 already separate these documents.
  • v2's Data Retention section is more granular (three specific categories: subscription, analytics, technical/security logs) vs. v1's one general sentence.

Open question: same as the Cookie Policy draft — the GDPR/UK GDPR framing and the GA4/PostHog naming reverse or extend calls made when v1 was built. If this reflects an actual decision (counsel-drafted, or a confirmed EU/UK launch plan and tech-stack choice), v1 should be rebuilt to match.
