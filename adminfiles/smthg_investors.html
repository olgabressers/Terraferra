02Pre-seed · €0.7–1.2M / $1–2M

Milestones before raising

  • Strong founding team: marketplace/product + fashion or creator economy + growth
  • Clear starting segment: independent designers, stylist-led shopping, occasion wear, modest fashion, or curated resale
  • 30–50 buyer interviews · 20–30 seller/creator interviews
  • 50–150 confirmed supply-side agreements (brands, sellers, or creators)
  • Prototype, concierge marketplace, or closed beta live
  • First 100–500 transactions or strong waitlist-to-purchase conversion
  • Commission, returns, payments, logistics, and seller liability understood

Targets to prove during pre-seed

  • 5–15K registered users
  • 1.5–4K monthly active shoppers
  • 100–300 active sellers / brands / creators
  • €50–150K monthly completed GMV by end of period
  • Take rate 12–20% if platform participates in transaction
  • ≥25–30% of quality listings result in a sale (transaction liquidity)
  • 90-day repeat purchase rate 25–35%
  • 25–40% of new users acquired organically or through creators
SMTHG cross-check · Year 1
SMTHG Y1 projects $2.16M GMV ($180K/month) with 18 active designers and 72 drops. This puts Year 1 GMV above the pre-seed end-target of €50–150K/month ($55–165K) — SMTHG enters at the lower Seed threshold if these numbers hold. The pre-seed check is: do we have 100–300 active creators and ≥25% transaction liquidity before raising?

SMTHG cross-check · Year 2
SMTHG Y2 projects $9M GMV ($750K/month) — above the strong Seed GMV threshold. Net revenue $1.35M/year ($113K/month) clears the strong Seed bar. The gaps to track before Seed: 90-day repeat (target 40%), transaction liquidity (target 35%), and organic/creator acquisition share (target 50%+). Active designer count 75 is below the strong Seed threshold of 1,000 creators — but SMTHG's model is designer-led, not creator-volume, so this metric needs qualification for investors.

What Seed capital should buy

  • Density in one primary market — not "all of Europe" simultaneously
  • Recommendations, social graph, and discovery improvements
  • Creator tools: storefronts, collections, video, affiliate attribution
  • Seller onboarding and catalogue automation
  • Return reduction: sizing, content, personalisation
  • First controlled test of a second city or country
  • Reproducible growth playbook proven across at least two cohorts or markets

What Series A capital should buy

  • Scale into 3–5 geographic or category clusters
  • International supply-side infrastructure
  • Recommendation engine and personalisation
  • Creator monetisation and affiliate network
  • Trust, authentication, returns, and fraud prevention
  • Senior growth, marketplace operations, and data team
  • Path to €100M annualized GMV — not just sustaining current business
05Social Commerce Loop · Must Measure

Adding a feed, likes, and profiles alone does not create a social-commerce premium. The investor needs to see a closed loop: content drives discovery → discovery drives purchase → purchase drives content. Every metric below is a link in that chain.

M-01
Content → product click-through rate
% of content views that lead to a product page
M-02
% GMV attributed to creators and UGC
Revenue directly traceable to creator or user-generated content
M-03
Viewer → follower → buyer conversion
Three-step funnel conversion rate end-to-end
M-04
GMV per active creator
Average monthly GMV generated per creator on platform
M-05
Creators with first sale within 30 days
% of new creators who generate at least one transaction in first month
M-06
Creator retention at 3 and 6 months
% of creators still active at 3M and 6M after onboarding
M-07
K-factor
New users per active user — organic referral multiplier
M-08
Recommendation vs search purchases
GMV split between discovery-driven and intent-driven purchases
M-09
Post-purchase content creation rate
% of buyers who create content (review, look, tag) after purchase
06What Moves the Round Size30–70% above or below base

Raises the round

  • Founder or creator owns an existing audience
  • 50%+ acquisition from organic, referral, creator channels
  • 90-day repeat purchase above 40%
  • Positive contribution margin before head-office costs
  • Fast-growing density in a specific fashion category
  • Exclusive or hard-to-replicate supply proposition
  • Proprietary data on style, sizing, and purchase intent
  • AI personalisation that measurably lifts conversion or reduces returns
  • Expansion playbook proven across at least two cohorts or markets
  • Multiple funds competing for the round

Lowers or kills the round

  • Calling it a marketplace while owning inventory
  • Growth driven primarily by discounts and paid social
  • GMV up but repeat purchases and contribution margin flat
  • Too many sellers with low transaction liquidity
  • No distinction between social engagement and purchase intent
  • Simultaneously launching in five countries
  • Team cannot manage fashion returns and quality control
  • Creator acquisition is effectively expensive influencer marketing
  • Take rate artificially inflated by temporary charges
  • Gross GMV, refunded GMV, and completed GMV not separated

Key principle: Each round eliminates one investment risk. Pre-seed removes product risk. Seed removes liquidity and retention risk. Series A removes scalability risk. Never raise the next round before the previous risk is visibly resolved.
