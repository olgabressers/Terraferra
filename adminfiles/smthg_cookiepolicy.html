SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG", "we", "us"), uses cookies to operate smthg, keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Services are used so we can improve them. This Cookie Policy explains how, and should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information and your privacy rights more broadly.

1. Overview

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Some cookies are set directly by us ("first-party"); others are set by a third-party service we use to operate or measure the Services ("third-party"). The cookies we use fall into two categories, described below.

2. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Services to function, including remembering your cookie preferences, keeping you signed in, and supporting core site navigation. They cannot be disabled, as the Services will not operate correctly without them.

3. Performance Cookies

These cookies count visits, measure site performance, and show us which pages are used most, so we can improve the Services. This information is aggregated and does not identify you individually.

We do not use advertising or cross-context behavioral-advertising cookies. Consistent with our Privacy Policy, we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies entirely, block only third-party cookies, or delete cookies already stored on your device. Blocking Strictly Necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working correctly.

We also provide a cookie preference manager, linked from the cookie banner, that lets you turn Performance cookies off while keeping Strictly Necessary cookies on.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under the CCPA/CPRA.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Where a cookie is set by a third-party service provider on our behalf, such as an analytics provider, that provider's own privacy and cookie practices govern the data it collects. We select service providers that use cookie data only to deliver the service they provide to us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised Policy here with a new effective date.

7. Contact Us

For any question about this Policy, contact us at contact@smthg.us.
