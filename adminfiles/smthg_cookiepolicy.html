This Cookie Policy explains how SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG", "we", "us"), uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on smthg. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

1. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies let a site recognize your device, remember your settings, and understand how the site is used. Some cookies are set directly by us ("first-party"); others are set by a third-party service we use to operate or measure the Services ("third-party").

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary (first-party). Required for the Services to function — for example, remembering your cookie preferences, keeping you signed in, and supporting core site navigation. These cannot be disabled, because the Services won't work correctly without them.
  • Analytics (third-party). Used to count visits, measure site performance, and understand which pages are most used, so we can improve the Services. This information is aggregated and does not identify you individually.

We do not currently use advertising or cross-context behavioral-advertising cookies. As stated in our Privacy Policy, we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will update this Policy and this section first.

3. Specific Cookies

Because the Services are still in development, this Policy describes the categories and purposes of cookies above rather than a fixed list of individual cookie names, providers, and expiration periods. We will publish that detail in this section once each specific cookie is deployed on the live Services.

4. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no common industry standard for how a website should respond to DNT, so — like most websites — we do not currently change our behavior when we detect one.

5. Your Choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block cookies entirely, block only third-party cookies, or delete cookies already stored on your device. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Services from working correctly.

Once the Services are live, we will honor a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request under the CCPA/CPRA, consistent with the rights described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Where a cookie is set by a third-party service provider on our behalf — for example, an analytics provider — that provider's own privacy and cookie practices apply to the data it collects. We choose service providers that use cookie data only to operate the service they provide to us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, including as we deploy new cookies to the live Services. We will post the revised Policy here with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us
