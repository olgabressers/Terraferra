This Cookie Policy explains how SMTHG Inc., a Delaware corporation ("SMTHG", "we", "us"), uses cookies and similar tracking technologies through smthg. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

1. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a site to recognize your device, remember your settings, and understand how it is used. Some cookies are set directly by us ("first-party"); others are set by a third-party service we use to operate or measure the Services ("third-party").

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary (first-party). Required for the Services to function, including remembering your cookie preferences, keeping you signed in, and supporting core site navigation. These cannot be disabled, as the Services will not operate correctly without them.
  • Analytics (third-party). Used to count visits, measure site performance, and understand which pages are used most, so we can improve the Services. This information is aggregated and does not identify you individually.

We do not use advertising or cross-context behavioral-advertising cookies. Consistent with our Privacy Policy, we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Should that change, we will update this Policy and add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism before doing so.

3. Specific Cookies

This section describes the categories and purposes of the cookies we use. For a list of specific cookie names, providers, and durations, contact us using the details in Section 8, or review the cookies set in your browser using your browser's built-in tools.

4. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no common industry standard for how a website should interpret it, so we do not treat it as an operative signal. This has no bearing on your privacy with us: we do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or behavioral advertising (Section 2). Your privacy choices are exercised through the browser controls and Global Privacy Control support described in Section 5.

5. Your Choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies entirely, block only third-party cookies, or delete cookies already stored on your device. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working correctly.

We honor a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request under the CCPA/CPRA, consistent with the rights described in our Privacy Policy.

We also provide a cookie preference manager, linked from the cookie banner, that lets you turn Analytics cookies off while keeping Strictly Necessary cookies on.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Where a cookie is set by a third-party service provider on our behalf, such as an analytics provider, that provider's own privacy and cookie practices govern the data it collects. We select service providers that use cookie data only to deliver the service they provide to us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised Policy here with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

For any question about this Policy, contact us at contact@smthg.us.
