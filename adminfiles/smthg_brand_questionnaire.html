Nine questions, sent to a brand after it leaves an email on smthg.us/for-designers. Six of the nine are a single tap. Three need typing: the brand name, the link, and the contact block.

01The questionnaire

Question 2 carries the decision. A website or Instagram link shows the product, the price, the photography and the audience in one click, which is what an onboarding call is otherwise spent establishing. The other eight questions exist to rule a brand out quickly on things a link does not show: where it ships from, whether it can produce to confirmed orders, and who handles returns.

02What each answer decides

Four answers can end an application on their own: a brand that sells wholesale only has no way to fulfil a drop, a brand shipping from outside the United States adds duty and delivery time to every order, a piece under $100 sits below the affordable-luxury range the platform is built for, and a brand that cannot produce to confirmed orders can only run the from-stock half of the model.
#QuestionWhat it tells usRules a brand out when
1Brand name Whether the label is already on the scouting lists, and whether anyone on the team has met them. Never on its own.
2Website or Instagram The product, the photography, the price list and the size of the following, in one click. This is the question the decision rests on. The work does not fit the curation, or there is no product to photograph yet.
3Where you ship from Duty, delivery time and returns cost for a US shopper, and which VAT position applies. Shipping from outside the US and UK or EU at launch.
4What you make Which creators to brief, and which Style Spaces the drop belongs in. Never on its own.
5Typical retail price Whether the brand sits in the affordable-luxury range, and what a 30% combined fee leaves on a unit. A typical piece is under $100.
6Pre-order capability Whether the brand can run the pre-order model, or only list from stock. Never on its own, but a stock-only brand is a narrower fit.
7Shipping and returns Whether the brand can fulfil a direct-to-consumer order at all. Stock, shipping and returns stay with the brand. The brand sells wholesale only and has never shipped to a consumer.
8Annual sales Which cohort the brand belongs to, and whether $250 a month is a serious number for them. Never. It is the one optional question, so a blank answer costs nothing.
9Name, email, WhatsApp A reachable person. A phone number moves the conversation to a call in a day instead of an email thread over a week. Never, but a missing WhatsApp number slows every following step.

Terms referenced above are the published ones on smthg.us/for-designers: 30% combined fee on GMV, $500/mo platform subscription, $250/mo for Founding Brands, community Space included.

03Build notes

Nine questions and one screen. Every question beyond the ninth costs completions, and everything an onboarding call needs beyond these nine is faster to ask on the call itself.
  • Six of the nine questions are a tap. Only the brand name, the link and the contact block need typing.
  • One page, no multi-step wizard, no progress bar, no account to create.
  • Question 8 is the only optional one, and it is the only question a brand may not want to answer.
  • WhatsApp sits beside email as a required field, so the first reply can be a call rather than a thread.
  • The form opens from a link in the reply to an email left on the site, so the brand has already raised a hand before it sees any questions.
  • Answers to questions 3, 5, 6 and 7 are the four that can close an application, and all four are a single tap.
