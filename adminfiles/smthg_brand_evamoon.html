01Stock integration

  • Status: answered on the call, 6 November 2026. Integration possible.
  • System: in-house stock management system built on Claude, made by Konstantin. Automatic, no Excel.
  • How it works: their own server reconciles stock every night.
  • Integration with SMTHG: their server has to be connected to the SMTHG brand account, so stock updates automatically on SMTHG too and SMTHG does not sell a piece that has already sold elsewhere. When: after the EVA MOON page on SMTHG is built and the content is delivered, and before the documents are signed.
  • Product files: if SMTHG sends its content requirements written as a prompt, Konstantin's internal system produces ready files for SMTHG to upload the products.
  • Later: Konstantin said he may partner with SMTHG to set up a similar system for creators.

02Brand and owner

  • Created on Koh Phangan. Tropical chic for free spirits who travel and live mostly on islands.
  • The brand has existed since 2019. Eva is the designer; Konstantin is co-owner and runs logistics and warehouses.
  • Style: boho chic for parties, festivals and yoga festivals. It is not street style, so creators must be chosen for this style.
  • Two audiences. US: mainly men, about $150. Thailand: women, about $300, natural silk.
  • Online sales are mainly the middle segment: men's models and couple robes. The website has 101 products, 54 of them kimonos and 16 kimono sets.
  • US clients are loyal and sales to the US are active. The $300 category is only starting in the US.
  • The headband is a separate brand of Konstantin's, left out for now.

03Sales, shipping and returns

  • Online shop on Shopify. Payment on the website: cards and PayPal.
  • US warehouse: delivery within the US takes about 5 days on average.
  • Items outside the US warehouse ship from Thailand by DHL, about 10 days to the US.
  • On the call: the website price includes all duties and delivery to the door in the US, for example $295 for the Eva Silk Kimono Red Coral.
  • The website shipping policy says buyers pay customs and import taxes, which differs from the call. Delivery there: 5–14 working days by DHL Express from Thailand or the US.
  • Website return policy: contact within 14 days of delivery, ship back within 30 days, buyer pays return shipping.

04Stock and production

  • The US warehouse is refilled periodically. Stock numbers per product were not given on the call.
  • Goods arrive at the warehouse packed and ready to ship.
  • Some products sit only in the US warehouse, so Eva cannot photograph them again herself.
  • Silk models are moving to natural Chinese silk, sewn in China. A model from the red kimono line is being sewn there now and will come in large quantity.
  • Warehouse in Europe planned, in Poland.

05Call 6 November 2026 – summary

  • Participants: Eva and Konstantin (EVA MOON), Maria and SMTHG. SMTHG presented the platform and the virtual try-on.
  • Terms explained: 30% commission on actual sales only, covering creators, payment fees and try-on generation. Founding Brand: $0 onboarding and $0 platform fee in the first year.
  • The brand keeps its own warehouse and logistics and includes all costs in the price. No stock is sent to SMTHG.
  • SMTHG is the seller, works on Shopify and takes the payment through Stripe. The brand ships and handles returns. Chargebacks and quality returns sit with the brand.
  • Payout every 45 days, after the return period. Returns: two weeks from receipt.
  • The first test covers the US only; Europe is blocked for now.
  • Launch steps agreed for EVA MOON: first, EVA MOON chooses the products and SMTHG builds the brand page; second, EVA MOON delivers the content; third, SMTHG and EVA MOON test the page and connect stock and logistics; fourth, both sign the documents and the brand launches. Target: November.
  • Maximum 6 products, visually consistent on one page. No very cheap products.
  • Selection: a few women's middle segment, a few women's high segment, men's middle segment in different styles. Both price categories are tested to see which sells.
  • SMTHG likes the Eva Silk Kimono Red Coral. It is not in the US warehouse and shipping it to the US now is unprofitable. Eva will offer a kimono from the same line.
  • Konstantin offered a ready file for upload from their internal system, if SMTHG sends the requirements as a prompt.
