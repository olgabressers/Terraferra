02Sales, shipping and returns

  • Online channels: own website (payments via Stripe) and Etsy. The website had 600 items before the range was reduced.
  • About 70% of shipments go to the US, with no US warehouse. She calculated a US warehouse after the new US duties and it did not work for a brand of her size.
  • Two delivery options to the US: DHL express, about 5 days; and a cheaper option, free for the client, 16–20 calendar days.
  • US shipments go duties paid by the brand (DDP), through Indian shipping companies whose package includes the duties and handles customs. DHL charges about $20 for duty processing alone; a shipment with duty comes to about $150.
  • Free shipping on the website from $350. On Etsy shipping is free.
  • Etsy collects EU taxes on EU sales. The brand has no US or EU tax number for its own website.
  • Returns: about 10%. Exchanges are handled. Experimental pieces are sold in the offline shop to keep online returns low.

03Stock and production

  • Bestsellers: up to 100 pieces in the warehouse. Other models: 4–5 pieces per size.
  • A workshop in India restocks continuously.
  • One-size bestsellers: ponchos and kimonos. Always in stock; ponchos suit a mild winter.
