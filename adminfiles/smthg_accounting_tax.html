02Ongoing Compliance Calendar
No quarterly filing obligation exists for a pre-revenue Delaware C-Corp with no payroll yet. Federal quarterly estimated tax payments (Form 1120-W) only apply once the company expects to owe $500+ in tax for the year — pre-revenue, that threshold is not reached. The obligations below are annual until the company has payroll or taxable income.
