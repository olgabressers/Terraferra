01Brand and owner

  • Knitwear brand. Anastasia owns the brand and knits pieces by hand herself, including the sweaters with holes.
  • Denser knits without holes: she makes the samples, production is outsourced to factories in Turkey and Russia.
  • She studied design in Moscow and continued in San Francisco, then started her own brand.
  • First contact: she met Maria's sister at a festival.

02Sales and distribution

  • 14 stockists worldwide: 2 in San Francisco, 1 in New York, 1 opening in Miami, plus London, Samui, Mexico and Bali.
  • Stockists work on a 50% / 50% commission. No own retail store.
  • Online sales only through her own website and Instagram. No marketplaces (no Wildberries, no Amazon).
  • Sales: 1 to 10 items per shop per month.
  • Turnover: $50K per year.
  • Global sales fell about three times after the Iran war.
  • Also sells at physical markets after Burning Man, such as Decompression.
  • Customers are worldwide, found in tourist places and at festivals, through word of mouth. At Burning Man most customers are from the UK and the US.
  • Almost no returns, because of the general sizing.

03Stock and shipping

  • No central stock. Small stock is spread across the world.
  • San Francisco: about 20 models, plus stock in the 2 shops there. Some stock in Los Angeles.
  • In the US now: 20 items. Possibly 20 more in shops in the US and London.
  • Parcels under 21 kg from Russia can be sent as personal items, without customs.
  • Shipping times: Turkey 1 week; Russia to the US 2 weeks; Russia to London 3 weeks.
  • US shipping costs $7 and is included in the price. For hats shipping may not be free.

04Production and lead times

  • Pieces she makes herself: 5–7 days.
  • Outsourced production of 100 identical items: 2–3 weeks (4 items per hour per machine). Mixed items take longer.
  • She can work on a drop basis.
