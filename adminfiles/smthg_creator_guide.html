How it works

From virtual try-on to your first sale.

smthg brings independent premium street style labels, creators and fashion lovers into one place. As a Founding Creator you are among the first: we match you with brands, you share them your way, and you earn from every sale you bring.

Your route
1

Join the founding community

Write to us. After a short call you are one of our Founding Creators.

2

We match you with brands

We pick independent premium labels that fit your style and your audience.

3

Get your link

Your personal link to the brand’s drop on smthg.

4

Try it on

Virtual Try-On from your phone. No samples, no shipping, no waiting.

5

Tell the story

Show the pieces your way. Talk about the label, its story and why you picked it.

6

Your followers try it on

They upload a photo and see the piece on themselves, from your link.

7

Earn 20%

From every sale that comes through your link.

8

Grow with us

Fashion events with the designers, producers and models behind the labels.
