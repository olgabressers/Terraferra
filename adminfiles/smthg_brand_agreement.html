Terms

This Agreement takes effect on the date SMTHG accepts the Brand's application, under Section 5.

1. Definitions

Products
The goods the Brand supplies to SMTHG for resale on smthg.
Customer
A buyer on smthg.
Drop
A fixed-window, pre-order sale event for the Brand's Products on smthg.
Retail Price
The price a Customer pays for a Product on smthg., set by the Brand under Section 7.
GMV
The gross value of all confirmed pre-orders in a Drop at Retail Price, before any Fees.
Platform Commission
The percentage of GMV shown in the Commercial terms. It covers the platform's share and all influencer involvement in the sale.
Purchase Price
The price SMTHG pays the Brand for a Product: the Retail Price less the Platform Commission.
Platform Fee, Onboarding Fee
The fees shown in the Commercial terms. Together with the Platform Commission they are the "Fees".
Statement
The monthly statement of Drop sales, Fees and amount due, available on the Brand Dashboard.
Return Period
The window during which a Customer may return a Product, set out on the Brand Dashboard.
Chargeback Period
The window during which a Customer's card issuer may accept a chargeback claim, up to 120 days from the transaction or delivery date under standard US card network rules.

2. SMTHG as Seller of Record

SMTHG sells the Products to Customers in its own name and collects payment from Customers directly. SMTHG is the seller of record for every sale made through smthg.

3. The Brand as Supplier

The Brand supplies the Products to SMTHG at the Purchase Price and fulfils each order directly to the Customer under Section 9. Title to a Product passes to SMTHG when the Customer pays for it.

4. Resale Authorization

The Brand authorizes SMTHG to resell the Products to Customers. The Brand grants SMTHG a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, reproduce and publish the Brand's product images, descriptions, names, logos and trademarks to list, advertise and promote the Products on smthg., on social media and in community content, and to let the influencers and creators working with SMTHG use the same materials for that purpose. The Brand keeps all intellectual property in its Products and materials.

5. Term, Suspension & Termination

The Brand applies through SMTHG's sign-up process and provides accurate business information on request. Once SMTHG accepts the application, this Agreement takes effect and continues for the Subscription Term, renewing automatically for equal-length periods unless terminated under this Section.

SMTHG may suspend or restrict a Drop if the information the Brand has given is inaccurate, or if a listing breaches this Agreement, SMTHG's policies or the law.

SMTHG may terminate this Agreement if:

  • the Brand has breached this Agreement, more than trivially or repeatedly;
  • the Brand has not paid an invoice by its due date;
  • the Brand becomes insolvent, or its ability to fulfil orders is at risk;
  • continuing the relationship could expose smthg. to reputational harm;
  • SMTHG decides to stop operating smthg. or stop selling the Brand's product category;
  • a tax authority notifies SMTHG that the Brand is not meeting its tax obligations;
  • the Brand breaches the SMTHG Code of Ethics; or
  • at SMTHG's discretion.

SMTHG gives at least 30 days' notice of termination, except where a legal or regulatory obligation, fraud, unsafe or counterfeit products, or a repeated breach requires SMTHG to end the Agreement sooner. The Brand may terminate on 30 days' notice, effective at the end of its then-current Subscription Term.

On termination: the Platform Fee for the current term remains payable; no Fees already paid are refunded; any amount the Brand owes SMTHG remains payable; SMTHG removes the Brand's listings within 14 days; the Brand still fulfils every order confirmed before termination; and SMTHG pays no compensation to the Brand as a result of termination or expiry.

6. The Services

SMTHG provides the Brand Dashboard and Drop infrastructure with reasonable skill and care, and aims for 24/7 availability, subject to routine and emergency maintenance. SMTHG keeps editorial discretion over which brands and Drops appear on smthg., to maintain a consistent standard of design quality. SMTHG may change the services on 30 days' notice; the Brand may terminate if it objects within that window.

7. Brand Obligations

The Brand is responsible for the security of its account and Dashboard access, and notifies SMTHG immediately of any suspected unauthorized use. The Brand keeps its product information accurate and current, abides by the SMTHG Code of Ethics, and does not expose smthg. to reputational harm.

The Brand does not include its own contact details, website links or any way for a Customer to reach it outside smthg. in its listings or shipments.

The Brand sets its Retail Prices, in consultation with SMTHG where SMTHG's quality-and-curation standards are relevant. The Brand does not price the same Product lower on its own site or other direct channels than on smthg. The Retail Price includes the cost of shipping to the Customer; smthg. shows no separate shipping fee at checkout.

8. Drops

smthg. holds no physical inventory. Production starts only once a Drop's window closes and orders are confirmed. The Brand sets the Drop window, item count and Expected Ship Date, measured from Drop close.

9. Fulfillment

The Brand ships every order directly to the Customer, within the number of business days from Drop close set out on the Brand Dashboard. The Brand is responsible for packing, shipping, delivery times and the carrier it chooses.

The Brand gives SMTHG the carrier and tracking number for every order through the Brand Dashboard as soon as the order ships, and within the same business day at the latest.

Every shipment includes an order-specific packing slip that shows the order number and names SMTHG as the seller.

10. Returns, Refunds & Defects

SMTHG answers to the Customer for returns and refunds. The Brand carries out the return and defect obligations set out on the Brand Dashboard: it accepts returned Products at the return address shown there, and replaces or refunds Products that are defective, wrong or damaged. Returned Products go to the Brand and are not sent to SMTHG.

When SMTHG refunds a Customer for a Product, the Purchase Price of that Product is deducted from the Brand's next Statement. If the Brand has already been paid for it, SMTHG may deduct the amount from a later Statement or invoice the Brand. A fee applies per returned order, set out on the Brand Dashboard.

The Customer bears the cost of shipping a return, and the Brand is not charged for it. The Brand bears the cost of replacing or refunding a defective, wrong or damaged Product.

11. Customer Chargebacks

If a Customer files a chargeback for any reason related to the Brand's Product or its delivery, the risk is the Brand's. SMTHG defends the chargeback through its integrated dispute system, and if the card issuer rules against SMTHG, the resulting loss is passed to the Brand.

12. Fees

The Brand pays the Onboarding Fee, the Platform Fee and the Platform Commission shown in the Commercial terms. The Platform Fee includes Stripe's standard processing fee; SMTHG does not charge it to the Brand separately.

Any additional fee not listed in this Agreement (including any fee for late fulfilment, cancellation or an incorrect item shipped) is set out on the Brand Dashboard before it applies. SMTHG may change Fees with 15 days' notice; the Brand may terminate within that window if it objects.

If the Brand wants an additional or custom agreement with an influencer beyond what the Platform Commission already covers, an additional fee applies, agreed with the Brand case by case.

13. Statement & Payment

Within 21 days of each calendar month's end, SMTHG posts a Statement on the Brand Dashboard covering GMV, Fees and the net amount due to the Brand per Drop. Payments are processed through Stripe.

SMTHG releases the payout for an order only once both the Return Period and the Chargeback Period for that order have expired, whichever is later. Until then the amount shows on the Statement as pending. SMTHG keeps withholding any amount or order that is still subject to an open chargeback or dispute until it is resolved. SMTHG may also withhold or offset a payout where it determines the account presents a fraud, chargeback or dispute risk.

If a chargeback, dispute or refund obligation exceeds the amount SMTHG then holds or owes the Brand, SMTHG may invoice the Brand directly for the difference, deduct it from a future Statement, or charge the payment method on file for the account.

14. Product Warranties

The Brand warrants that it owns or has the right to sell the Products and their intellectual property, that it is authorized to sell them, and that they are safe, of satisfactory quality and comply with applicable law. Product safety, labelling and manufacturing compliance stay with the Brand, and the Brand provides compliance documentation on request.

15. Data Protection

SMTHG is the data controller. The Brand is the processor for Customer order and delivery data it receives to fulfil a shipment. The Brand uses that data only to fulfil the order, does not solicit the Customer directly or include its own promotional material in the shipment, and securely deletes the data 30 days after dispatch.

16. Indemnity & Liability

The Brand indemnifies SMTHG against losses arising from the Brand's breach of this Agreement, a defect in the Products, an intellectual-property infringement claim, or a third-party injury or property claim connected to the Products.

SMTHG is not liable to the Brand for indirect or consequential loss or lost profit. SMTHG's total liability to the Brand under this Agreement is limited to the Fees the Brand paid to SMTHG in the 12 months before the claim arose.

17. Sales Tax

SMTHG collects and remits sales tax on sales made through smthg. to Customers in the US, as marketplace facilitator, calculated per order based on the Customer's delivery address. Where a sale is made to a Customer in the EU or UK, SMTHG is responsible for VAT as the deemed supplier for that sale. The Brand is not responsible for collecting or remitting tax on sales made through smthg.

18. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential, disclosing it only to those who need it to perform this Agreement or as required by law.

19. Dispute Resolution

Either party may request mediation before escalating a dispute. If mediation does not resolve it, the dispute is resolved by binding arbitration, on an individual basis. Neither party may bring or participate in a class, collective or representative action.

20. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

21. General

SMTHG may amend this Agreement on 30 days' notice, except where a regulatory or urgent security or fraud reason requires an immediate change. If the Brand disagrees with a change, it may terminate before the change takes effect; continuing to use smthg. after that date means the Brand accepts it. Neither party is liable for a failure or delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable control. The Brand may not assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without SMTHG's consent. This Agreement, together with the SMTHG Code of Ethics, Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy, is the entire agreement between the parties.
