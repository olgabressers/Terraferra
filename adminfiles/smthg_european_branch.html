European Branch
SMTHG INC. · Delaware C-Corp · CEO tax-resident in the Netherlands · 3 ways to give SMTHG a European legal presence.
DECISION: REGISTER FOR VAT ONLY. DO NOT OPEN A BV OR BRANCH OFFICE YET.
Key Terms
KVK
The Dutch Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) — every business operating in the Netherlands must register here.
BV
Besloten vennootschap — a Dutch private limited company. Its own legal entity, separate from its owner, roughly equivalent to a US LLC/Inc.
Branch Office
Nevenvestiging — a foreign company (like SMTHG Inc.) registers itself directly at KVK, without creating a separate new legal entity.
PE
Permanent Establishment (vaste inrichting) — a real, ongoing physical presence (staffed office, shop) that makes a foreign company taxable in the Netherlands.
CIT
Corporate Income Tax (vennootschapsbelasting) — Dutch tax on business profit.
UBO
Ultimate Beneficial Owner — the real person who owns/controls a company; must be registered with KVK.
VAT
Value-Added Tax (BTW in Dutch) — the EU sales tax charged on goods/services.
OSS / IOSS
EU VAT schemes that let a business file one return covering multiple EU countries; IOSS is specifically for low-value imported goods (≤€150).
Kantoor Buitenland
The Dutch Tax Administration's dedicated desk for non-resident (foreign) businesses.
Apostille
An international certificate confirming a document (e.g. SMTHG's incorporation papers) is genuine, needed to use it in another country.

Recommendation
  • Officer appointment (Blocker below) is on the critical path for both BV and Branch Office regardless of which is chosen — resolve that first.
  • Cheapest path to any legal EU presence for SMTHG Inc. itself: Branch Office, not BV — €85.15 KVK fee, no notary cost, no share capital, days not weeks.
  • Branch Office carries full parent-company liability (no ring-fence) — a BV limits exposure to the BV's own assets, at the cost of the notary fee (~€500–1,500) and ongoing accounting (€800–3,500/yr).
  • VAT-only is the cheapest option with zero entity, but Wolf & Badger's structure shows the real end-state at scale is a multi-entity setup (UK, EU, US, AU each contracting separately) — VAT-only is a starting point, not necessarily the permanent shape.
  • Given no immediate EU sales volume, VAT-only or Branch Office are the lower-cost ways to keep the option open without committing to BV-level cost before there's revenue to justify it.
Blocker

Branch Office and BV both require a signature from a director of SMTHG Inc. — KVK explicitly does not accept a lawyer, notary, or authorized representative for this declaration. SMTHG has no board-appointed officer yet (see Legal — Before the Pre-Seed Round Closes, Action 1). That appointment has to happen first if either is chosen.

How It Works
QuestionAnswerConfidence
Does KVK registration itself create Dutch tax liability?No. KVK automatically shares your details with the Tax Administration (you get an RSIN/tax number) — that's a data hand-off, not a ruling that tax is owed.Official — business.gov.nl
What makes the branch a "permanent establishment" (vaste inrichting)?Triggers PE: a shop/fixed sales point, a workshop/factory with an office, the place the business is actually managed from, a staffed branch conducting real operations.
Does not trigger PE: a storage/goods depot, purely supporting functions (admin, R&D, advertising, information-only), short-term/incidental activity.		Official — Belastingdienst
If it is a PE, what tax applies?Standard Dutch corporate income tax — 19% up to €200,000 profit, 25.8% above. Same rate/base as a BV — no separate "branch tax." A PE also has no dividend withholding tax exposure, since there's no subsidiary paying dividends up to a parent.Official rate (KVK) + Medium (no-separate-tax claim)
Does the branch file annual accounts with KVK, like a BV must?Generally no — this is a BV-specific obligation. Exception: if SMTHG Inc. were itself legally required to publish financial statements in Delaware, that filing duty could carry over to the Dutch branch. A private Delaware C-Corp files an annual franchise-tax report + officer/director list, not financial statements — so this exception likely would not apply, but that inference is not independently confirmed by a Dutch lawyer.Medium, with one Unconfirmed inference flagged
Is VAT registration automatic once the branch exists?No — separate step. The branch registers as an independent VAT taxpayer once it has PE/taxable activity; it isn't bundled into the KVK appointment.Medium
Can the branch hire staff, run payroll, sign contracts, open a bank account?Yes to all. Hiring requires registering as an employer, Dutch wage-tax withholding, Dutch-law employment contracts. Bank account opening is slower for a foreign-parent structure due to KYC on directors/UBOs — one source cites ~65 days, not officially confirmed.Medium, banking timeline Unconfirmed
Does the US–Netherlands tax treaty help with double taxation?Yes, a treaty exists (1992, amended 2004). Its Article 5 defines permanent establishment, explicitly including "a branch." Relief is generally via foreign tax credit — Dutch CIT paid would offset US tax owed on the same income, not exempt it outright.Medium — treaty exists (official, IRS), article-level detail from secondary summaries
